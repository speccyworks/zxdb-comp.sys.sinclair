Hiya...

First of all, make sure that the value you are changing is for lives.. Do
this by starting the game so you have 3 lives, look at the value of the LD
(NN),a. Return to the game, and die once. Look at the value again. If it
has changed to the correct number, then huzzah!

What you should then look for is something like

LD a,(NN) - 58,n,n
LD hl,(NN) - 42,n,n
LD hl,nn - 33,n,n

Those are the most commonly used ones...

Sources too numerous to mention give 35136,0 as the infinite lives poke
for the original Bug-Byte version of Manic Miner. For the later Software
Projects version, Your Spectrum 14 (May.1985) gives 35142,0 instead.

Poke 36160,0 gives not so many meanies and 36106,0 gives immunity,
according to Sinclair User 53 (Aug.1986) and Your Sinclair 35 (Nov.1988),
but neither states which version they relate to.

Your Spectrum 18 (Sep.1985) also gives an infinite air poke for both
versions:
Bug-Byte
34795,195: 34796,241: 34797,135: 36233,195: 36234,149: 36235,141
Software Projects
34801,195: 34802,247: 34803,135: 36244,195: 36245,160: 36246,141

plus, according to Your Sinclair 1 (Jan.1986) the Bug-Byte version also
needs: 34798,0: 34799,0: 34800,0 for the infinite air poke to work;
although it doesn't say whether a similar modification is needed for the
Software Projects version - add 6 to those three addresses and check what
would be NOPped out, I guess, if you've got the SP one.

Oh, look, here's another one (SU.24, Mar.1984); apparently POKE 34269,N
will give N lives in the Bug-Byte version.

That's your lot.
