CSSCGC entries checked against ZXDB

About this list

The comp.sys.sinclair Crap Games Competition (CSSCGC) ran in the newsgroup from 1996, and ZXDB has one tag per contest year listing its entries (1996–2026; none for 2019). On 25 Sep 2026 every post about the contest (2,453 posts, 1996–2026) was read and compared with those ZXDB lists. The goal: find entries, authors, real names and hosts that the group shows but ZXDB lacks. Nine helper agents each read a few contest years, and Claude validated and spot-checked the results (see "How this was checked" at the end). ZXDB copy: ~/projects/zx-spectrum/zxdb/zxdb.sqlite of 18 Sep 2026. The same findings as data: csscgc-checked.tsv.

In short: ZXDB's contest lists are very complete for most years. Where an organiser posted a full list (1996, 1997, 1999, 2007–2010, 2014), every entry is in ZXDB. The real gaps:

1999: the judge reviews an entry called "Arcade Emulator" (1999-06-15). His list has 42 games and so does ZXDB's; the only ZXDB entry not on his list is Advanced Test Failure (30613), and his description ("your favourite arcade game is now pushing up the daisies because of it's corrupted ROM") fits it. It is probably an alternative title.

How this was checked
Posts: the index's contest posts, plus 156 opening posts that mention the contest only in their text, 2,453 in all. Only each post's own text was used (quotes removed). They were grouped by contest year and given to nine helper agents, each with ZXDB's list for its years and read-only access to ZXDB.
What each agent did: listed every game the posts show as entered, matched it loosely against ZXDB's list, searched all of ZXDB for anything not found, and noted authors, real names and hosts.
Validation: all 162 result rows were checked. Every cited post, entry and label exists, and for 155 of them the quoted evidence was found word for word in the cited post. The other 7 cite From: addresses or combine two quotes.
Re-checked by Claude, independently: every "missing" title was searched again in ZXDB. The near misses are other programs: Mazeman, a 1985 Ladron, the book type-in Old Bones, and ZX80 Mastermind entries by other authors. The key posts were read again: the 1998 May list, the 2006 ZX80 Mastermind, the 2012 diving game, the 2005 host and the 2021 final list. Every "no author" claim matches ZXDB. Every handle pair was looked up in ZXDB's label links; four the agents reported as new were already linked, and are left out.

The verdicts are judgements, not rules: build_index.py does not rebuild them. csscgc-checked.tsv keeps all 162 rows with their Message-IDs and thread files.
