Inky $tring Zofftware is proud to announce:

the CASCARA CASSETTE 10 - a compilation of quality games, hours of fun
for all the family!

Side 1 of the tape features:

Advanced Weapons Inspector Simulator: take on the role of
lederhosen-wearing UN bigwig Hans Blix, in this *Historically
Accurate* subterranean adventure.

Who's at the Door?: a day in the life of a jobless layabout - will you
interrupt your daytime tv viewing to answer the door?

Vulcan Bomber: fever-pitch excitement as you take the controls of a
crippled V-wing aircraft.

Quandary: based on the ever-popular board game, pit your wits against
the computer opponent. "Better than chess" said Anatoly Karpov [1]

Super-Maze: Simply a-maze-ing! Special edition features bonus 'Dark
Dungeon' level

Available now from all good electronics boutiques &
alt.binaries.comp.sinclair

Side 2 coming soon!
