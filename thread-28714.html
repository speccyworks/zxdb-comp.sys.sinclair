Hi all...

CRONOSOFT.co.uk is announcing a BRAND NEW
NEVER SEEN BEFORE Spectrum release...

************************************
>>> *** ROUGH JUSTICE ***
>>> by Jonathan Cauldwell
************************************
A slick arcade shooter/puzzler, with
a great 128K soundtrack.

PRICE: £2.99 + shipping
Order online at http://www.cronosoft.co.uk
with PAYPAL in GBP / Euros / US$
(or manually with NOCHEX in pounds)

***********************************

Here's the storyline...
Intergalatic bounty hunter and cyborg, Mansfield Rough, vanished while in a
remote part of the galaxy over a month ago, and nothing has been heard of
him until now.

A message demanding a huge ransom has been received from a mystery alien
race inhabiting the hitherto unexplored planet, DRAGONIA-6809.

His niece, Shipley, a chip off the old block, and having just obtained her
pilot's license, decides to head off and teach those nasty aliens a lesson.

Mission:

Navigate Shipley's craft, the Interceptor 010, throughout cavern after
cavern - avoid contact with aliens - blast crates to reveal bounty within
them (some contain treasure, adding to your score, while others increase
firepower).

Time your moves to dodge forcefields and be wary of rockets that launch at
you. Your weapons are a laser, which will destroy rockets, crates and
certain aliens. You also have a bouncing bomb which will destroy everything
in it's path.


+----------------------------------------+

Thanks

Simon
Cronosoft.co.uk

like to see, but after it's releassed Jonathan Cauldwell plans to put
updates, and patches to extend it on his site for free, but you'll need the
original.
