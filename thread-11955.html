4 posts, 1998-04-23 – 1998-04-24

1998-04-23 andrew broad ZXDB: Manic Miner 6: The Buddha of Suburbia
I have just released a new Manic Miner game, THE BUDDHA OF SUBURBIA, on
the Internet! It can be downloaded from:-

http://www.cs.man.ac.uk/~broada/spectrum/download/

A thousand thanks to Richard Hallas for rescuing it off tape for me!

I have also updated my list of MM/JSW games
(http://www.cs.man.ac.uk/~broada/spectrum/list.html) accordingly,
including a mention of Ignacio Perez Gil's Manic Miner 5 and Adam
Britton's Special Edition of The Continuing Adventures, both of which
are expected to be released on WOS tomorrow (hopefully The Buddha Of
Suburbia, the 1998 reissue of Manic Miner 4 and my Manic Miner Screen
Editor too?). What an exciting week this is for MM/JSW fans!!
