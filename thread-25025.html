All you'd need to do was search for Robocop on WoS and look in the SPOT*On
section of the results for 'M' entries, where you'll find something like
this:

  M C   4.89  p. 32
  M SU  2.89  p. 12
  M YS  4.89  p. 40
  M YS  5.89  p. 34
  M YS  6.89  p. 27

You can download the full SPOT database from my website, then you can
search for information such as this off-line.
