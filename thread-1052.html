Just to annouce that Sinclair Scene, the
Sinclair/Spectrum section on the Monochrome BBS is now
open. In fact it has been open for about 2 months, but
we've been setting up for a while. We hold copies of
emulators for most major systems, a few PD snapshots,
some home made (ie appallingly crap) games, and a few
hints/tips/solutions/pokes (but we want *more* !).

For people without accounts on Mono, to log in, Telnet to
ELECTRON.CITY.AC.UK and log in as MONO at the first
prompt, and then as GUEST at the second prompt. We can be
found on the "Data Bus", key path [S] [D] [Z].

We also have an FTP site BSP208.STAFFS.AC.UK which anyone
can use (login as ANONYMOUS). We carry the emulators plus
a few other files there.

Come and visit us soon !
