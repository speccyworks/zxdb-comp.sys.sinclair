Yes folks - the comp.sys.sinclair crap games compilation is FINALLY HERE!
(phew!)

I've finished playing about with it and I'm now confident enough to put it on
show in public (oo-er). Attached to this posting is an advert (I know adverts
aren't really 'Net-esque', but it is free and it is our own product) to give you
just a brief glimpse of what you can expect.

The full thing is already available from NVG as :
ftp://ftp.nvg.unit.no/pub/sinclair/snaps/games/misc/CSS_Crap_Games_Comp.zip

...and will hopefully be available from Demon and the NVG mirrors sometime in
the near future. If you were a contibutor, I'm about to mail out copies to you -
they should be with you by tomorrow morning.

You may notice that it's a little bigger than anticipated...! The truth is I
just couldn't pick 50 entries, 60 was the best I could manage. I hope you're not
all disappointed! ;-)

I've organised the games as .SNA files, so with a bit of luck everyone should be
able to play them on their favourite emulator.

So, that's that then.
All that's left for me to do is to thank everyone who has helped and hope you
all have a good laugh at the games. Strange but true, I've actually found myself
going back to a couple of them to seriously play them.... I think I've been at
this too long!

All comments VERY gratefully received!


Blood, the crap-meister.
