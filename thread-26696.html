2002-12-02 10:05:12 Andrew Broad ZXDB: Goodnite Luddite
I have just released my new Jet Set Willy game, the long-awaited sequel to We 
Pretty: GOODNITE LUDDITE!

It's the best game I've ever made: atmospheric, visually attractive, technically 
sound, packed with quirky features, fun to play, and pleasant to explore.

Goodnite Luddite comes with a not-so-short story written up in the form of a 
diary, which I think the reader will find both exciting and funny. It's very 
surreal, set in a Lynchian nightmare-world with its own peculiar logic - it's a 
more coherent story than We Pretty.

Goodnite Luddite is set in a Selesian monastery (today being Monica Seles's 
birthday), and you have to exorcise the spirit of Kari Krisnï¿½kovï¿½ before the 
world ends at midnight! (Yes, this JSW game really does end at midnight, unlike 
the original JSW which ended at 1am! ;-) )

It's not my most difficult JSW game in terms of exploration, although it does 
require knowledge of the quirks of the game-engine 
[http://www.cs.man.ac.uk/~broada/spectrum/willy/features.html] to see more than 
ten rooms. But it's probably my most difficult JSW game to complete in terms of 
collecting all the items. I say it has the best geography and the best 
difficulty-gradient of all my games.

Goodnite Luddite is available today for downloading from my website:
http://www.cs.man.ac.uk/~broada/spectrum/download/


I have also reissued all of my previous Spectrum software with updated 
documentation (the TAP files are completely unchanged). I have fixed the 
newlines so they should display correctly on PCs, edited the Internet sections, 
copyright notices, &c. to make them up-to-date and consistent with each other, 
and made various minor changes.

The products that have been reissued are the following:
+ Manic Miner 4
+ Manic Miner: The Buddha Of Suburbia
+ We Pretty
+ Manic Miner: The Hobbit and Jet Set Willy: The Lord of the Rings
+ Manic Miner Screen Editor
+ Jet Set Willy Construction Kit version 0.0 (public beta version)
+ MM:JSW and JSW:MM (public beta versions)
+ Jet Set Dizzy (public beta version)
