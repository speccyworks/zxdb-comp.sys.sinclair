I'm afraid this game is renowned as one of the most unplayable ever
released for the Spectrum; in particular, having to rewind and reload
after failing on any level soon became unutterably tedious - although this
aspect is made slightly more tolerable when using an emulator (unless
you've got it on TZX).

Anyway, here's the instructions from the Encore re-issue (repeatedly
taking off my glasses so I can read the microscopic print about an inch
from my nose, and then putting them on again so I can see the screen to
type). You'll see why you're having a problem on the 2nd level - although
I'd expect that, despite the instructions, you'll be no closer to
resolving it. I never got past the second level - but I was playing it
back in 1986 on a real Spectrum, so the continual re-loading soon did for
me (I bought the original Software Projects release, but that was stolen
some years ago, so I've only got the Encore tape now).

""
You earn an extra life for completing the Weapons Room, and one for each
screen completed, up to a maximum of five. To record your high score press
the space bar when on the title screen and you'll be able to enter your
initials.

Order of screens: 1 Falling Disk; 2 Skull Hallway; 3 Burning Ropes; 4
Weapons Room; 5 Ramps & Giddy Goons; 6 Tentacle Room; 7 Second Disk; 8
Deadly Checkerboard; 9 Slaying the Dragon.

Either keyboard or joystick may be used simultaneously:
Z	Left	Move left
X	Right	Move right
K	Up	Move up
M	Down	Move down
L	Fire	Draw sword -or- Jump
Space		Jump on Giddy Goon

When playing the Ramps & Giddy Goons screen you will need to press SPACE
to jump from ramp to ramp. In Skull Hallway, Weapons Room and Tentacle
Room, the joystick will only accept the correct action at the correct
time; the correct action at the wrong time will be ignored. If you hold
the joystick in anticipation of a move it will be ignored, resulting in
losing a life. 

To use your sword just press L to draw and slash with the sword (at the
correct time). You always have your sword with you except on the final
screen where you will need to collect it from the top of the cliff before
slaying the dragon.
""

""
FIRST DISK
The POKEs should get you through this tricky first section without much
trouble (see below).

SKULL HALLWAY
Continually tap forward on the joystick to avoid the skulls. Press the
fire button to crush the first hand, and tap forward to avoid the second
set of skulls. Press fire to destroy the two hands - one comes from the
left, the other from the right. Pull back on the joystick to avoid the
bats, and then push right to avoid them. Push left to elude the slime,
then forward to shun it when it comes back again, and finally right to
escape from it for the last time.

BURNING ROPES
Push up and keep your finger on the fire button until you reach the third
stone. Always wait until the rope nearly touches the stone before jumping
onto it. When on a rope, swing twice before jumping to the next.

WEAPONS ROOM
Continually press fire to dispose of the ball and chain. Push left to
avoid the jug. Press fire to push back the wall. Push right to dodge the
axe and then right again to eschew the shield. Press fire to push back the
other wall. Push left to shirk from another axe. Press fire to dispose of
yet another shield. Push forward to elude another axe. Push right to
escape from the fireball before you exit.

RAMPS & GIDDY GOONS
Kill all the goons or you will not be able to jump to the next ramp. The
ramps disappear after a while so don't linger.

TENTACLE ROOM
Continually press fire to dispose of the tentacle from the ceiling. Push
left to move away from the tentacle which attacks from the floor, then
push forward to evade another. Press fire to dispose of the two tentacles
which appear one after another from the ceiling. Push right to move closer
to the door, then right again to dodge another tentacle before you exit.

SECOND DISK
No POKEs for this one, I'm afraid, but it's easier than the first disk
anyway. The wind doesn't blow as hard, but it gusts for twice as long and
appears at least eight times.

DEADLY CHECKERBOARD
Keep jumping onto a square next to a knight, hitting him with your sword
each time.

DRAGON'S LAIR
Get the treasure if possible. Be careful as you go for the sword -
especially at the edge of the cliff. Watch out for Singe's fire. Jump off
the ramp and Daphne is yours.
""


POKEs for First Disk (I haven't tested this, but there was an obvious typo
in line 40 (30803 instead of 40803) which I've corrected):

40 FOR f=40794 TO 40803: READ a: POKE f,a: NEXT f
50 RANDOMIZE USR 30720
60 DATA 175,50,43,206,50,155,202,195,48,184
