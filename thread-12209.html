Hi gang,

Just thought I'd bring you a quick update on how the CSSCGC's coming
along. :)

So far we've had approximately 30 entries which I haven't disqualified
and I've disqualified the following:

which I didn't even bother to decode and play

it later is neither here nor there ).

And now it's over to Mystic Meg to predict the winner so far:

This years cgc winner will be a man...or a woman. The letters E and L
appear somewhere in the persons name and the game in question will be
