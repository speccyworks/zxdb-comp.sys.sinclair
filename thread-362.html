I've played Manic Miner again for the first time in ages.
Still an absolutely great game. I was quite surprised to
find that I still remember the cheat for it.
If anyone wants to know - simply type the number 6031769.
A boot should then appear next to the lives.
Now - by holding down 6 with a combination of other keys
allows you to play any of the twenty screens.

E.g. 61 is the second screen, 62 is the third screen,
621 is the 4th screen, 63 is the 5th - I'm sure you
get the idea.

Happy Mining,
Brett.
