Here's what I've been playing with this afternoon:

http://www.chuntey.com/eightyone/sp81.zip

It's a ZX Spectrum emulator (kind of) for the ZX81. It works fine in
EightyOne, but will probably need a a bit more work to get it going on a
real '81. Unfortunately, Load/Save and Beep don't work yet, but I might have
the urge to play with it some more tomorrow.

A big thanks to Geoff Wearmouth btw for his excelent ROM disassemblies on
which this is based.
