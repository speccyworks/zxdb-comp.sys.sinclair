********************************************
*		EMULATE No.5               *
********************************************

I am glad to announce that Emulate No.5 is now out, albeit in a 
slightly slimmed down version - I am hoping that by reducing the size 
of the magazine slightly, I can release an issue more regularly, 
hopefully issuing one every month.

Don't worry - There is something else with this months edition - The PC 
EXECUTABLE version on Emulate!!!

If you have a PC - you MUST see this!! It includes screenshots, 
pictures, plus loads of articles, all in an easy to read PC format!!!

Of course, there is still a text version for other computer users, but 
I advise seeing this on a PC - I'm really proud of it! :)

PLEASE!!! Let me know what you think of the EXE version, either on this 
newsgroup or by posting personally.
The EXE magazine is in two parts and needs 550k free base memory to run.

You can find all the different EMULATEs on Demon (once they are moved 
out of the incoming - probably by Monday)

FTP.DEMON.CO.UK    /pub/emulators/spectrum/magazines/emulate

EMULATE5.ZIP   -  The text magazine
EMULSNP5.ZIP   -  The snapshot pack
EMULEXE5.ZIP   -  The PC Executable version

Enjoy the magazine(s) and I really want to hear for ALL of you who read 
it!  Yes - YOU!!!
