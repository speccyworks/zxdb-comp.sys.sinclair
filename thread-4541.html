* EMULATE! ISSUE 2 IS NOW AVAILABLE *
*************************************

After a slight delay, issue 2 of Emulate! The Spectrum
Emulator magazine is now available.

The magazine includes these great features:
REVIEW GUIDE Part 2 (B)
SPECTRUM GAMES CHARTS + COMPLETE GUIDE TO SPECTRUM CHARTS
PLAYING TIPS AND ADVENTURE GAMES
SPECTRUM HISTORY Part 2
TECHNICAL SECTION
COMPLETE INSTRUCTIONS FOR GAMES
GUIDE TO DOOMDARKS REVENGE SAVED GAMES
SEPCTRUM DATABASE RUN-DOWN

Also available are the following games, in a seperate games pack:

Two EXCLUSIVE Games!
JOIN THE JET SET - Excellent JSW Game by Richard Hallas!
STREET HAWK - Never before seen Pre-Release version of the game.

Plus:
THE BARDS TALE - The complete 17 Level Role Playing Game.
ROCKSTAR ATE MY HAMSTER - Complete TAP file, including ALL pop
                          stars for you to choose from.
LOADS OF MIDNIGHT - Two Part Lords of Midnight satire.
RETARDED CREATURES AND CAVERNS - Excellent Zenobi Adventure.
EVERYONES A WALLY - The second Wally Arcade Adventure.
16/48 No.3 - March 1984 issue of the tape magazine.
SPECTRUM TESTER - Spectrum tester program by our technical editor.

This great magazine will be available from the NVG UNIT archive, as 
soon as I can get it to talk to me!! At the moment, I am having 
problems sending it to the site. Anyway, Once I manage to "make 
contact" you will be able to find the mag at:

FTP.NVG.UNIT.NO/pub/sinclair/incoming
The filenames are:
EMULATE2.ZIP - The magazine itself
EMULSNP2.ZIP - The Games Pack, including all above games.

I will send out copies of the text mag to those who send me a 
request, as usual.

ENJOY THE MAG!!!
