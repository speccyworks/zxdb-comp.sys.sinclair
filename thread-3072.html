Spanner - wards off Frankensteins monster
Cross - wards off Dracula

Also, you can go through certain objects depending on which character you are
i.e. the Serf can go through barrels, the Wizard can walk through bookcases
and
the knight can walk through Grandfather clocks.

You will require the yellow key to find the 3rd part of the main key. This can
only
be found by falling through a trap door.

My record was 1min 55seconds to solve the whole game.
