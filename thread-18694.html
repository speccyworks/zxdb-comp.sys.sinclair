The solution was printed in Crash issues 31-33 (Aug-Oct.1986), so someone
obviously finished it.

I never played the game myself - Avalon was enough for me, and Dragontorc,
Astroclone and Pyracurse were basically more of the same (although
Pyracurse wasn't by Steve Turner), but harder, so I couldn't be bothered
with them. (Pyracurse was still rather ahead of its time, in fact, as the
gameplay is much more like Where Time Stood Still than the Turner games,
and WTSS came out two years after Pyracurse). If you get the TAP off WoS,
you can try one of these pokes:

[from Crash 32 (Sep.1986) - infinite lives]
10 CLEAR 24799
30 LOAD "" CODE 23296
40 POKE 23325,201: RANDOMIZE USR 23299
50 POKE 33446,201: RANDOMIZE USR 29600

[from Your Sinclair 12 (Dec.1986) - infinite energy]
10 CLEAR 24799: LOAD "" CODE 23296
20 POKE 23325,201: RANDOMIZE USR 23299
30 POKE 33450,201: RANDOMIZE USR 29600
