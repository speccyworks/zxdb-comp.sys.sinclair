Coincidentally (unless it was by you) this question was raised just a few
weeks ago, but I don't recall if it was resolved. Whenever anyone mentions
The Hobbit I tend to stop taking much notice. Check on Deja News
(http://www.deja.com/usenet/). There were maps of the Goblin's Dungeon
printed in Sinclair User (Aug.83) and Your Sinclair (May.87), but I've
never seen mention of one for the whole game. It's more hassle struggling
with the bugs in the game than keeping track of where you are.

I've still got my hand-drawn map from when I played the game back in 1984,
but I've just had a look at it and it's a great scrawly mess spread over
four pages, with arrows going all over the place; and although I finished
the game I wouldn't want to try and produce a legible map from my jumbled
notes.
