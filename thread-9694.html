In article <5qhf68$…@…>, "Alvin R. Albrecht"  wrote:
>You must have been some glutton for punishment.

Hmm... :-)

Well, things I have disassembled in my time include:
 - the Interface 1 ROM (which I typed on a manual typewriter, so scanning
   in is a possibility if/when I get a computer)
 - the Currah ï¿½Speech ROM
 - JSW, Chequered Flag and Doomdark's Revenge
 - +3 ROMs 0 and 1 (plus the bits of 3 which are different from the normal
   48K ROM)
and I started doing the Sam ROMs but never finished them.

The games, obviously, I did so I could cheat by knowing how they worked
and/or by poking them; I also defined new levels for JSW and Chequered
Flag.  The Interface 1 Rom was rather useful - it made it possible to
write lots of programs that deal with microdrives and so on at a low
level.  The other ROMs were similarly done in order find out how they
worked and how to use them (and why the +3 editor is so full of bugs!).

I didn't have to do the +3ROM because it is pretty well documented in
the +3 manual, and anyway at the time I wouldn't have had a clue how
the disk hardware worked.
