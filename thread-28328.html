There are advantages to PNG other than its license. Its compression
generally gets filesizes smaller, it can handle truecolour images up to
48bits (GIF hits its limit at 8bit palettes), it has 8bit alpha channels
compared to binary transparency in GIF, colour correction with gamma
correction, chromacity or a colour management systems, 2D interlacing
compared to GIF's 1D, and probably better file integrity checks. Maybe 
some other advantages too.

I don't think most people give two hoots about the licensing issues.  
Plenty of people still use GIFs without having paid anything.

One thing certainly holding back PNG (other than the blind ignorance and
mule-like stubbornity that prevents people from using something better
IME) is IE on PCs. I'm using IE6 here[1] and it *still* doesn't render
PNGs with alpha channels properly. What the hell are MS doing with all
their R&D cash if they can't replicate what most other browsers (free or
commercial) have, including their own IE on MacOS!!
