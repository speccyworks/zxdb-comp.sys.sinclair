2000-01-19 andrew broad ZXDB: Willy Takes a Trip
Geoff Eddy has just released his latest JSW game, Willy takes a Trip.
It is now available for downloading at:
http://www.cix.co.uk/~morven/jsw.html

I beta-tested this game, and definitely recommend it to all MM/JSW fans. It
was just short of brilliant when I beta-tested it, but now it's got a load of
patch-vector effects in it, and might well make that mark! :-)
