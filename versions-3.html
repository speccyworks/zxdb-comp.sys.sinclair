Release dates and versions checked against ZXDB

About this list

Release announcements in comp.sys.sinclair often give the day a program or piece of hardware came out, its version number and where to get it. On 25 Sep 2026 the announcements tied to a ZXDB entry (98 posts, 65 products) and the releases ZXDB lacks (59 posts) were read and compared with ZXDB's release dates and download files. Three helper agents did the reading, also searching the rest of the group for later versions. Claude validated and spot-checked the results (see the end). ZXDB copy: ~/projects/zx-spectrum/zxdb/zxdb.sqlite of 18 Sep 2026. All rows as data: versions-checked.tsv.

How this was checked
The posts: the 98 announcements linked to a ZXDB entry (by the rules with a score of 0.5 or more, or by the earlier hand check) and the 59 checked as missing from ZXDB. They were grouped by product, with ZXDB's releases and download names for each.
Checking: three helper agents read every post and searched the index for more version posts by the same authors. They decided the real release day (not a preview or a re-release) and the versions, and compared both with ZXDB (read-only).
Validation: all 111 rows are present, and every cited entry and post exists. Every release date equals the date of its post, except Avalanche and Mayhem, which use the event date the post gives. Claude checked the main claims against ZXDB and the posts: the Emulate issue dates, ZXZVM, The Time Hole, Avalanche/Mayhem, BBC BASIC, Chopper Drop 3000, zblast 81, QWord and the +3e ROMs.
Mechanical data: every announcement's versions, links and file names (not checked) are in the index table announce_files, which versions.py builds.
