ZXDB's date is often only a year. The group shows the day the release was announced, or the day the post says it came out.

Corrected 25 Sep 2026: eight entries were first listed here as "no date" (Riptoff 2, Peter J. Mella's three games, Professional Coat Getting Simulator, Len's Chuntey Challenge, Tunnel Escape 2, Crap Invaders, Jump!). They have no date of their own, but ZXDB records them as first released inside a CSSCGC compilation that has a year, and Spectrum Computing shows that year (e.g. Jump!: 2004). The "ZXDB date" column now gives that year. They still belong in this list, since ZXDB has only a year; for Riptoff 2 and the three Mella games the group's day (1999) is in the year before the compilation (2000), and for the Coat Getting Simulator (2001) the year after. Only PipePanic has no date at all.
