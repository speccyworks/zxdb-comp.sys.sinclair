Tipshop games checked against ZXDB

About this list

The Tipshop (https://www.the-tipshop.co.uk/) posted its monthly updates to comp.sys.sinclair from 2003 to 2021. Each update credits who sent in POKEs, maps, solutions and tips for which games. The index reads those credits and links each game to ZXDB. Until 25 Sep 2026, 421 credits (341 different titles) named a game the index could not find in ZXDB. This list says what they were. The same verdicts as data: tipshop-titles.tsv. ZXDB copy: ~/projects/zx-spectrum/zxdb/zxdb.sqlite of 18 Sep 2026.

In short: nearly all of them were the index's own reading errors or spelling differences, not games missing from ZXDB. 12 games are really missing. (Two titles the checks could not settle are left out; they are in tipshop-titles.tsv.)

How this was checked
The rules in tipshop.py and linking.py were fixed first (see the index README, "Tipshop credits"), which left 187 unmatched titles.
Three helper agents took about 62 titles each, with the sentence from the update. They searched ZXDB read-only, including ZXDB's links to the Tipshop's own pages (webrefs, website 9), whose page names are the Tipshop's spellings.
Their 187 verdicts were validated: every title present, and every cited entry exists.
Claude checked a sample of 25 "under another name" verdicts, and searched again for every "not in ZXDB" and "unsure" title, joining words and ignoring punctuation. Nothing was found.
Claude settled 9 titles that appeared after the rule changes.

The verdicts are judgements, keyed by Message-ID and title as written in tipshop-titles.tsv. tipshop.py reads that file on every build, but only for titles its rules cannot match.
