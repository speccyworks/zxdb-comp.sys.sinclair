Search engine fixes, and more cheats/maps/tips than a horse has legs!
(Eh? Ed)

* Updated the alias section of the search function, as it wasn't always
inserting line breaks properly. It was also not displaying search
results if the title had an apostrophe in it (very handy!). It
should(?) hopefully behave a bit better now.

* In order to get Tipshop more in line with WOS' Infoseek, I've updated
the names for 1985 - The Day After, 3D Construction Kit, 4 Soccer
Simulators, ACE 2: The Ultimate Head to Head Conflict,
Archaeologist, The, Avior, Batman, Biggles, Black Knight, The, Chopper
X-1, Garfield - Big, Fat, Hairy Deal, How To Be A Hero, Intruder Alert
2: Invaders from Planet X, Ivan 'Ironman' Stewart's Super Off Road
Racer, Maze Death Race, Oink!, Pasteman Pat, Peter Shilton's Handball
Maradona, Pi-In-'Ere, Prehistoric Adventure, Traz, Tres Luces de
Glaurung, Las War Cars Construction Set, Wizard of Akyrz and Yabba
Dabba Doo!. Thanks to Martijn van der Heide for sending me the list of
Tipshop mis-names.

* Removed some direct links for tips for some Load 'n' Run games. In
case you didn't know, these were english software titles illegally
translated and renamed by a Spanish/Italian magazine. If you do a
search for them, you should be shown the original english title instead.
I'm working through the rest of them. Thanks to Martijn van der Heide
for giving me a note of these.

* Paul Carter got in touch to say we'd joined two of his tips from Your
Sinclair together. And he was right. So we now have an extra tip for
Tracksuit Manager - hurrah!

* Added a map for The Train: Escape to Normandy, scanned by Thomas
Franke. This is the map which originally came with the game.

* Added maps for Covenant, The, Mr. Wino, R-Type, Tarzan Goes Ape,
Thundercats, Tour De Force, Trashman 1 and Uridium Plus and Uridium by
Ladderman.

* Added a map for Jet Set Willy 5: ZX Hero, by MadMekon.

* Added maps for Ice Temple, The, Joe Blade 1, Joe Blade 2, Joe Blade 3,

for the 48K version only). He also sent

the MASTERTRONICS mistake, for one thing). Fortunately, Viktor provided
POKEs for both versions - hurrah!
