Happy 26th birthday Allana!

Today I release my new game, Manic Miner: Neighbours - Allana Truman
(MM:N-AT), a tribute to my favourite character ever to grace the great
Australian soap-opera, and a playable visual interpretation of the
plotlines involving her and boyfriend Lance Wilkinson. The game also
serves as a more general patch-study of Neighbours as it was in 2001.

It's an `oldskool' platform-game for the Sinclair ZX Spectrum,
emulators for which are freely downloadable from the Internet:
http://www.geocities.com/andrewbroad/spectrum/

It's based on the game-engine from Matthew Smith's classic game from
1983, Manic Miner, where you have to collect up to five items from
each level before advancing to the next, whilst avoiding various
deadly hazards and solving physical puzzles. The basic controls are
left, right, jump... and pause!
[ http://www.geocities.com/andrewbroad/spectrum/willy/ ]

I consider MM:N-AT to be the best of the five Manic Miner games I've
written, as it features such brilliant rooms (IMHO) as "Spread The
Word", "Dr. Karl Kennedy's Surgery" and "Sci-Fi Collectibles". It's
not as mind-bogglingly difficult as my previous Manic Miner game Ma
jolie, as I set out with the goal of making it fun to play.

But it's certainly tough by ordinary standards, so I have included an
EASY VERSION of the game which reduces the need for saving
memory-snapshots every few seconds, whilst retaining most of the
quirky features such as jumping through innocent-looking wall-blocks
and having to relieve items of crumbling floor, and fun manoeuvres
such as stopping on a conveyor so that you can jump straight up.
[ http://www.geocities.com/andrewbroad/spectrum/willy/features.html ]

MM:N-AT is the most visually inspired of my Manic Miner and Jet Set
Willy games to date, as I have explored the idea of designing some of
the rooms as playable pictures - "The Coffee Shop" and "Lassiter's
Lake" being prime examples.


MM:N-AT implements significant modifications to the Manic Miner
game-engine, all of which are documented so that competent
Spectrum-hackers will be able to reuse them:

1. It's the first new game to incorporate John Elliott's patch which
allows any room in a MM game to have vertical guardians, Eugene, Kong
Beasts and switches, Skylabs, and solar power.

2. I have transferred the Jet Set Willy item-collection sound to the
Manic Miner game-engine.

3. You play as a different character in "Dr. Karl Kennedy's Surgery"
(like in "The Nightmare Room" of Jet Set Willy).

4. I have fixed the bug which tramples over memory (notably Room 7)
when you fall off the bottom of the screen or jump up off the top.

5. The in-game tune is played at half-speed.

6. I have fixed an undesirable feature in colouring vertical guardians
(their colour-attributes are no longer ORed with the background
colour-attribute).

7. I have altered the end-of-game swordfish-routine so that the player
no longer appears above the portal, and his legs are erased properly
from under the portal (i.e. to the left as well as directly below).
