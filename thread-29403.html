Today I release Part 1 of my new Jet Set Willy game, Party Willy - so
called because it's in several parts, most notably two 48K JSW parts
(Part 2 will be released on 23rd April), which I will combine to
produce a single JSW128 game (hopefully by 23rd April).

Party Willy is released as a box-set consisting of the following:

* Party Willy itself: the brand new game from Broadsoft, featuring
reinterpretations of the original Jet Set Willy rooms (and a few from
other sources), revamped versions of 27 old rooms that I wrote in
1993, and many brand new rooms. I feel I have captured much of the
atmosphere of the original JSW, whilst maintaining the Broadsoft
standards of technical innovation. It is long overdue to return to the
art and craft of quirky features, that's what this game is about.
School's in session.

* The old Jet Set Willy: Manic Miner conversion which I wrote in 1993:
the 20 MM caverns, plus 27 rooms of my own, which have been revamped
and included in Party Willy itself.

* The new Jet Set Willy: Manic Miner: a semi-automatic conversion of
MM to JSW, now with just the 20 MM caverns plus "Master Bedroom", but
with guardians, proper items, and other gubbins.

* Manic Miner: Jet Set Willy: a three-volume hand-conversion of JSW to
MM which I wrote between 1993 and 1996, and of which I have now edited
other aspects (most notably the Room 19 pictures).

* reniM cinaM: a laterally-inverted version of MM, generated by a
program called MirrorMM which will be released in the future as part
of Version 1.3 of my SPECSAISIE toolkit. I've even worked out how to
edit the switch-behaviour in the Kong-Beast rooms, and mirror the
solar beam!

* ylliW teS teJ: a laterally-inverted version of JSW, generated by a
program called MirrorJSW which will be released in the future as part
of Version 1.3 of my SPECSAISIE toolkit.

Party Willy is available today for downloading from my website.
