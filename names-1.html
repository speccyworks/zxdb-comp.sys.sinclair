Real names and handles checked against ZXDB

About this list

The group often shows that a handle and a real name are one person: the same address posting under both, a signature, or someone saying so. ZXDB records such links between its labels (a Person label, Nickname labels, linked by owner/from). On 25 Sep 2026, 145 candidate pairs from the group were checked against ZXDB's labels. Only pairs that touch a ZXDB label, and that ZXDB doesn't already connect, were kept as candidates. ZXDB copy: ~/projects/zx-spectrum/zxdb/zxdb.sqlite of 18 Sep 2026. The same verdicts as data: names-checked.tsv.

This list adds to the 20 contest authors in section 5 of csscgc-vs-zxdb.md (deKay = Andy Kavanagh, Woody = Mark Woodmass, Dr. Sputnik = Gavin Callard and others). Those are not repeated here.

Pairs the posts could not settle, and warnings about labels that look alike but are different people, are left out of this list (25 Sep 2026); they are in names-checked.tsv.

Most candidates were rejected, and for good reasons. Several addresses were shared placeholders used by many people (…@…, …@…, …@…), and university accounts were used under other students' names. A match to a ZXDB label was often a namesake: the 1995 York student posting as "Dave Hughes" is not the 2011 author Dave Hughes (R-Tape).

How this was checked
Candidates (145 pairs), from three sources:
names used on one address (the index's posters table), skipping truncated usenetarchives.com addresses;
"X aka Y", "real name" and "Name (Handle)" in post texts and From: lines;
the index's poster-to-label matches from posted entries.
Filtering: a pair was kept only if one name equals a ZXDB Person or Nickname label (a lone common first name only for nickname labels with entries), and ZXDB doesn't already link the two.
Checking: three helper agents judged about 48 pairs each. They read the posts and signatures in the index and checked ZXDB (both read-only), including whether the ZXDB label is the same person or a namesake.
Validation: all 145 verdicts are present, and every cited post and label exists. Claude read the key sentence of every finding in its post, and confirmed in ZXDB that none of the handles and real names in sections 2 and 3 exists as a label, and that the pairs in section 1 are unlinked.

These are judgements, not rules; build_index.py doesn't rebuild them. names-checked.tsv has all 145 pairs with Message-IDs and thread files.
