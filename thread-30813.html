The comp.sys.sinclair Crap Game Competition 2004 is over! (Whaddya mean 
you didn't know it had started?)

w1n0rz with his lazy rushed incomplete effort "Falling", due to loadsa

from the drop-down list (in retrospect, I should've left the top

Chris Young which was the only game that failed to receive a single 
vote! Coming last in the CGC has to be an achievement, dontcha think?

You can now download all 69 games in one handy zip, so go geddit...

www.speccyspoilers.co.uk/cgc2004/

Many thanks to all of you that entered a game - the general standard of 
programming was abysmal so give yourselves a slap on the back.  :)
