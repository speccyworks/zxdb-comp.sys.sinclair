An updated search script, and the tips cross the 10,000 mark! 

* Yep, you read that correctly - we now have over 10,000 tips - HURRAH!
A massive
thanks to all the Good Eggs out there who have helped make this
possible!

* I've been dabbling with PERL again, and have implemented a new
feature to the
search script. If you search for a game which was re-released (or
known) as some
other title, it SHOULD(!?) find the original title, and display the
tips for that.
For example, searching for Gunsmoke should bring up the tips for
Desperado 1.
The data for this was kindly supplied by SpecDeity Martijn van der
Heide - thanks,
as always, to him for taking the time to supply this.
If you find titles which don't work, please let me know (supplying both
titles,
preferably)


* Viktor Drozd has obviously had a few sleepless nights, and has sent
in a huge batch
of stuff: an update note for his POKEs for Abe's Mission: Escape, the
solution to
part 1 of Capitan Trueno, POKEs for 750cc Grand Prix, Alien Storm, 
Assassin, BC Bill, Beach Head 1, Beverly Hills Cop, 
Bobby Yazz Show, The, Butcher Hill, Camelot Warriors,
Captain Blood, Castle Master 2: The Crypt, CJ's Elephant Antics,
Double Dragon 2: The Revenge, Eagle, Energy Warrior, Extreme, 
Explorer, Flunky, Forest Of Doom, Future Games, NARC, 
Night Hunter, Samurai Trilogy, SDI (Strategic Defence Initiative), 
Shadow of the Unicorn, Skull & Crossbones, Sonic Boom, 
Superbike Trans-Am, Techno-Cop and White Heat; cheats for 
Blazing Thunder, CJ's Elephant Antics, Predator 2, 
Superbike Trans-Am and Wild West Seymour; and tips for Forest Of Doom
and Night Hunter (PHEW!!!)

* As well as that load, Viktor also pointed out a few more POKEs which
hadn't ported
over from Hack Attack III. Namely: 720 Degrees, Extreme, Hysteria,
Rygar, Split Personalities and Uridium

* Daren Pearcy sent in a correction to one of the complete solutions to
Castle Master 1.
He also passed on details of the new RZX walkthrough for Dark Side, The
by
Starglider which you can download from the RZX Archive.

* Martin Ball sent in POKEs for the Flash Beer trilogy

* Chris Thompson sent in his solutions to Atlas Assignment, The, Black
Tower 1 and
Project-X: Micro Man 1

* Added maps for Head Over Heels, Pentagram, Willow Pattern Adventure,
The by Sherwin Robb

* Corrected spelling for Assassin - thanks to Viktor Drozd for pointing
out the typo!


PHEW!!!
