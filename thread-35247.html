Yesterday :-)

First of all, every game has been voted by 4 judges in 3 categories:

1.- Overal crapness (10 = maximum crapness, a winner; 0 = minimum
crapness, a loser).
2.- Funny factor (10 = makes you laugh your ass off; 0 = boring).
3.- Odd factor (10 = the most strange; 0 = quite common).

Category 1 was used to choose the WINNER (highest score - the most
crap) and the LOSER (lowest score - the best crafted).
Categories 2 and 3 are just for the aknowledgement.

So the WINNER iss Richard May's Random Walk with an overall crapness
score of 8.5 out of 10.
The LOSER is Arda's Drawlander with an overall crapness score of 1 out
of 10.

Special categories:

The FUNNIEST game is JBGV's Uchidanza with a whoppin' 9.25!
The MOST STRANGE game is Apenao's Game of (musical) thrones with 8
points.

It's been a great compo, thanks to everyone who entered and made it
possible!

Visit the site to grab everything -> http://www.mojontwins.com/csscgc2011
