The TZX tape format

Every thread in the group about the TZX tape file format itself, first called "ZXTape" or "the new ZX Tape format": its proposal by Tomaz Kac in 1996–97, the updates and revisions of the specification, its homepage, how its blocks work, extensions and questions about reading and writing it. Threads that only use TZX files (uploads, the TZX Vault, emulators, conversions) are not included.
