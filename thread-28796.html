2003-11-24 16:50:35 Russell Marks ZXDB: Speccy Minigame Collection
I finally finished my collection of almost all the speccy and ZX81
entries from all three years of the Minigame Compo, and it's available
here:

  http://rus.members.beeb.net/minicoll.html

(I uploaded it to WoS as well, so it should be available there soon.)

It's a 128-only single load, and lets you switch between the games
without having to reset or anything. As for the ZX81 games, they're
ported/emulated in the same way as my other ZX81-on-speccy releases.

Thanks to all the minigame authors who agreed to let me include their
games. And if anyone who knows them could ask Wocen or Stefano Bodrato
if I could include their games (in a future version) that'd be
helpful; they didn't answer the emails I sent.

-Rus.

What I prepared for this year's votepack was just to help people 
unfamiliar with the Spectrum to try and vote the games. Since it was for 
voting, I had to be sure that the menu system didn't leave traces of 
itself once a game has been loaded, and also that everybody could easily 
check that. How useful an unappealing menu can be for people here that 
can load each game separately?

Clearly Russell didn't have such "voting" limitation for his 
compilation, and he made a really great job. I hope that having all 
games together in a nice menu system convinced people to try them, and 
maybe they may decide to partecipate to or vote at next year's - this 
year now - competition (voting is easy and doesn't take too much time).
