2004-03-02 16:27:35 Dunny Re: CSSCGC2003 Results CSSCGC Crap Games Contest 2003 ZXDB: Nightmare Park
The Starglider wrote:

> I don't think the next competition should be based on a public vote,
> since it seems no-one understands what they are meant to be voting
> for - THE WORST GAME!

Bollocks.

The whole point of Cascade's cassette 50 (with FREE calculator watch) was that
many of the authors had written games which they *believed* to be good attempts
at games. The fact that they were part of the biggest collection of gaming dross
ever assembled in one place was incidental to those authors.

So I voted for the people who had *tried* to produce a game. I certainly
wouldn't vote for anyone who attempted to produce a crap game. I wouldn't vote
for anyone who tried to pass off an in-joke as a game. I'd not vote for anyone
who had written a small BASIC stub and called it a game when they *knew* it
wasn't. You know who you are.

If Cassette 50 was part of the compo, I'd vote for the best of that lot -
probably something like Frogger or another well-attempted game. We shouldn't
reward people who produce pointless shite.

Rant over, carry on.

Oh, and Woody's entry was one of my faves - a crap game, but well attempted.
BadBeard's Nightmare Park was possibly the the best Cassette 50 style game too.
It was great fun to play, and well thought out.

D.
