No solution, but here'a a few tips.

POKE 34252,0 - infinite lives
(Paul Hutchinson, Crash 16 (May'85))

ditto, or POKE 30394,n + 33969,n (n = number of lives)
(K Savage, Sinclair User 52 (Jul'86))

POKE 34208,0 - infinite lives
or
POKE 30394,n + 33969,0 (n = number of lives)
(Tom Dalziel & Paul MacPherson, Your Spectrum 16 (Jul'85))
[The 30394 poke defines the number of lives available, and the 33969 poke
the number displayed on the screen. If the latter is greater than four the
screen display will get messed up.]

To get rid of the cat (and leave the castle):
"First of all collect the magic flame, which can be found in the cold
upper maze - just look for the purple door. A bit further up is a green
door - go through that and buy some sulphur from Anne the trader.
Next go to the slimy lower maze; above you should be a gold door. Through
this is Gort the trader's room, where you must buy some charcoal and a
transmat key. Go to the transmat room and get teleported.
Now go back to the big puss without boots and drop the charcoal and
sulphur on the saltpetre, then drop the magic flame on that little bundle,
and the cat'll blow up!""
(James Whitfield, Your Sinclair 25 (Jan'88))

philosopher's stone + iron bar = gold bar
pile of mud + spark of life = mud monster
empty bottle + model of Cutty Sark = ship in a bottle
broken sword + blacksmith = Excalibur
(Marc Kavanagh, Sinclair User 40 (Jul'85))

To get to the gold door:
"First go to the cold upper maze, to the bottom left and down the hole.
You will be magically transported to the bottom left of the Level One
maze. So follow the wiggle monster until it goes back on itself again.
From then on go left and you'll soon get to it, but never set out without
魹ｽ200 in cash 'cos you'll have to trade that for the transmat key."
(John Robertson, Your Sinclair 44 (Aug'89))

That's your lot.
