Z80 tape interface and the Oric

3 posts, 1999-08-30 – 1999-09-02

1999-08-30 19:26:39 jim groom 
Hi all, 

The following is the text of a message I have posted in comp.sys.oric,
but as it has some relevance to this group (and people here are more
likely to be aware of what I am blabbering about) I thought I'd post it
here too. So, if anyone can help with the below, please get in touch,

"I was wondering about the possibility of using the tape interface which
is for use with the Sinclair Spectrum emulator 'Z80' by Gerton Lunter.
This device plugs into your PC Parallel Port at one end and you plug
your cassette recorder leads into the other (connected to your trusty
tape player of course). You can then load your Spectrum tape into your
emulator as if you had a real Speccy with a tape recorder. You can also
record the tape image and save that too. Recording out to tape is also
allowed (& works well). If anyone is interested, a diagram of this
device is supplied with the Z80 program (shareware or registered
version). If possible, it would be nice if someone could find out some
way of using this interface to get programs into & out of Euphoric onto
real cassette tape. Can it be done? Is anyone out there willing and able
to make this happen? I believe this interface may also be usable with a
Dragon emulator, so how about the emulator community standardising on
this device as the standard bridge between the 8 bit emulators and tape
recorders? 
I would welcome everyones thoughts on this matter."

Regards,

Jim
