resolution. MY wav player can play 8-bit PC mono .wav files at 47kHz in
4-bits, and I could make it up to about 60kHz if the pre-processing was
already done. Mind you to fit them into the Speccy is the harder bit.  the wav
player is on nvg, something like snaps/utils/wavplayer.zip

I did 2 versions - one more for fun which has code to pick up the wav info
(speed,sample size etc), the other more like a programming tool for coders to
call.

Yeah - that was to me.  But I decided to write
a version that played sound files of its own
format:  two 4 bit samples are packed into a
single byte and there is some simple
compression (like z80 files).

It relies on SOX and a small dos program
to convert mono and stereo sound files
of just about any format to the format
for the sound player.

This one will play mono samples between
1 and 54kHz and stereo samples between
1 and 24kHz.
