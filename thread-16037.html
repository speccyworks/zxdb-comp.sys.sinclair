1999-08-30 17:52:03 address hidden CSSCGC Crap Games Contest 1999 ZXDB: 1D Tetris
Channel 4 has a late night programme about computer games called Bits.
In one link on the 19th August, one of the presenters said:

"For the rest o' you, SimPornIndustry is not in the works, so stop
sending it in. But do keep sending in your home-made games: we got one
feom one mailer, curiously called Sonic Sweet Pea, who sent in The Penis
Game -- very droll. Send 'em in though, because we're hoping to review
them at the end of the series."

I see no reason why we cannot submit some of *our* home-made games for
review. (I missed CSSCGC '99 because I was so disappointed with the
handling of CSSCGC '98.) The prospect of having some slow, badly written
BASIC programs (running on a 1982 computer) reviewed on a show designed
for Nintendo 64 users is particularly appealing to me. I for one will be
submitting my Advanced Wanking Simulator, Advanced Revive The Holocaust
Simulator and Advanced Fuck The Skull Of Jesus Simulator, along with my
cow-throwing game and 1D Tetris.

I'll send them an explanation of what the CSSCGC is, which Spectrum
emulator to get and what to do with .tap and .z80 files, so all you need
do is send in your favourite programs. The address is
