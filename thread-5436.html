RIGEL'S REVENGE - THE SOLUTION
==============================

This solution does not include all the commands you need to
complete the game. You must examine & pick up things you find
and find your own way around at least!

PART ONE
--------

1.  FIND GOGGLES, WEAR GOGGLES, EXAMINE BODY for satchel (to PUT
    things in). Also GET RUBBLE.

2.  In the desert, GET BONE & RETRACE TRACKS.

3.  In hotel, EXAMINE DOOR, UNTIE WIRE & OPEN DOOR. LOOK UNDER
    BED, PUSH BED (twice) and GET SHAPE.

4.  In suburbia, CLIMB LAMP & go eastwards. GET SCREWDRIVER, GO
    EAST, THROW RUBBLE AT LIGHT, WEAR GOGGLES, GO EAST, GET
    RIGELLIAN UNIFORM & go back to bridge.

5.  In kitchen of flat, EXAMINE SINK, GET MEDIKIT & WEAR MEDIKIT.
    GO UP, PULL BAR HARD, PUSH RED BUTTON, BEND BAR, DROP ALL
    THROUGH WINDOW (including what you're wearing), GO NORTH,
    GET MEDIKIT, WEAR MEDIKIT, PUSH GREEN BUTTON.

6.  By mound, CLIMB MOUND, GIVE BONE TO DOG, WAIT, CLIMB MOUND,
    GO WEST, GO SOUTH.

7.  At gap in footbridge, PUSH GREEN BUTTON & JUMP GAP.

8.  From robocoptor, GO EAST, ENTER TANK, PRESS SWITCH, GET MINE
    DETECTOR, PRESS SWITCH & GO UP.

9.  In crater, GET BOMB & CAREFULLY CLIMB UP!

10. At edge of No Man's Land, WEAR UNIFORM, WEAR HEADPHONES, 
    TURN DIAL (twice).

11. At hut, INTRODUCE BOMB.


PART TWO
--------

1.  REMOVE GOGGLES, GET BOMB, GO NORTH, DROP BOMB, GO SOUTH,
    WAIT & GO NORTH.

2.  In hut with apparatus, INSERT GUN, PULL LEVER, GET GUN.

3.  At east end of Plaza, LOOK UNDER BENCHES & GET CAN.

4.  WAIT at west end of Plaza until soldiers march past, then
    go to vehicle. GET RIFLE, LOOK UNDER SEATS, GET DOCUMENTS &
    EXAMINE DOCUMENTS for identcard.

5.  At dustbins, EXAMINE DUSTBINS, OPEN CASE & GET FLARE.

6.  Wait for troops & follow them through checkpoint (with rifle &
    uniform).

7.  In copse, DIG for lightguide. PUSH MEMORY for info.

8.  At bridge, SHAKE CAN & SPRAY CAN AT LENS.

9.  At gate, INSERT IDENTCARD.

10. At wall, EXAMINE WALL, EXAMINE SHAFT, UNSCREW GRILLE & CRAWL IN.

11. CRAWL along until you find the opening to the lab. SHOOT SOLDIER
    WITH GUN, PUSH MESH & CRAWL NORTH.

12. EXAMINE CUBE, OPEN PANEL, PUT GUIDE IN SLOT, PUSH SWITCH 1, PUSH
    SWITCH 3, PUSH SWITCH 4, PUSH EXECUTE & WAIT until sphere arrives.
    GET SPHERE and CLIMB INTO SHAFT.

13. Crawl to opening to power room. PUSH MESH & CRAWL NORTH.

14. OPEN TRAPDOOR, GO DOWN & WEAR GOGGLES.

15. When monster appears, PULL RIPCORD on flare.

16. Find Japu and you've done it!
