Okay, here we go...

recently I sent my DRiVER disc back to Revelation -- namely because it's 
bugged to hell.

FLAME ON ==-*

Here's a problem: why don't they beta-test their software? It'd take all of 
15 minutes (because that's how long it took me to find 4 major crippling 
bugs in it). Yet Carol Brooksbank did a glowing review in Format... how 
could she? Did she actually USE the program? Jeez.... I dunno... the way SAM 
releases are going these days I am seriously thinking of just packing it all 
in and moving to a sensible computer [1] like the Oric Atmos -- at least you 
know where you are with them...

FLAME OFF ==-~

I sent it back. They checked the disc (duuuuhhhh... I knew it wasn't the 
disk -- it was the software). I now quote the letter I got:

(I'll post the letter I sent them on here tomorrow)

[letter begins]

Revelation Software		PO Box 114, Exeter, Devon, EX4 1YY, England
    			
Mr S.Cooke,
18, Braemer Drive,
Sale,
Cheshire,
M33 4NJ						23rd February 1994.

Dear Mr Cooke,

	Please find enclosed your master disc for DRiVER which you returned 
for testing. I have had Format Publications (who do our disc duplication) 
test the disc and verify it against the duplication master copy. From this 
we have confirmed that there is no fault with the disc.

	Turning now to your rather lengthy comments on the DRiVER system I 
can now make the following replies:-

	1. The icon corruption bug is known and will be corrected in version 
1.1 due out April/May. However this does not affect running of the program.
	2. We have not heard of this problem before and have passed your 
comments on to the author for him to investigate.
	3. This problem has been reported by one other user but so far we 
have been unable to reproduce it - investigations will continue.
	4. Again this is a problem we have not heard of before but we have 
asked the author to look into it.

	We are also passing your list of recommendations on to the author 
although I doubt if many could be implemented because of the limits imposed 
by space. However, in the meantime DRiVER is still a very useful and usable 
system that is gaining many devoted fans. A disc of additional applications 
programs, called DRiVER EXTRAS, will be launched in the next few months and 
I understand that FORMAT plan some in-depth articles in the near future.

	Details of how to obtain an upgrade will be published in FORMAT and 
FRED when the new version of DRiVER is announced.

			Yours Sincerely,

			Frank Broughton,
			For and on behalf of
			REVELATION SOFTWARE.

[end of letter]

Ignoring the obvious points about stupidity (how is it possible to misread a 
PRINTED address on a letter? My address is 18 braemAr drive,not braemEr and 
my postcode is M33 4NJ -- not M22 4NJ), the more fun bits come in now...

First of all I don't get FRED or FORMAT (my "lifetime free sub" to FRED 
disappeared when FRED became a large money-making concern), and I've never 
bought FORMAT, although I do get to read other peoples copies... So if I don'
t get them, how can I see how to get the new version of DRiVER?????

Secondly, "We doubt if many could be implemented because of the limits 
imposed by space."

To put it bluntly, this is bull-shit. There's a 512k memory map out there 
waiting to be used, and the DRiVER system code has 32k to play with (base), 
but there's nothing to stop it taking up and extra 16/32/64K if necessary. 
So that's just smoke-screen.

"FORMAT plan some in-depth articles in the near future."

Oooh, that's really nice... I want some little bitty articles, when what I 
could do with is a PROPER technical manual, given on disc FREEEEEEEE to 
programmers... If I have to pay, sorry, I won't support it. I can't afford 
to buy my programming team a manual at 15 quid a shot, especially if the 
program it's for is slow and bugged.

"Driver is still a very useful and useable system"... to do what, may I ask? 
The notepad is slow and bugged. There's no other software on the disc that 
can be used (unless you want to play a slidy-puzzle or draw more icons). Of 
course, we could spend HOURS playing on File-Manager, couldn't we???

Here's the biggie:

The envelope was not posted from Devon, not from Exeter... where it WAS 
posted from was GLOUCESTER.

I don't know if many people know this, but it seems to be general knowledge 
in some circles that Revelation Software is owned/run by FORMAT. There is no 
such software company in Exeter -- it's just Big Bad Bob behind it all.

No wonder it never got beta-tested.

Nuff said, rant over, response please.

Simon Cooke
