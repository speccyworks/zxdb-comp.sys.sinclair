2005-06-06 19:35:32 deKay CSSCGC Crap Games Contest 2005 ZXDB: Manic Muslim
Just in case any of you don't read the WOS forums, I'd like to fill you in on
an email I received yesterday.  Here it is:

-----------
Dear Sir

Please remove or re-name the above software ASAP.

I find it extremely offensive that you have decided to
tarnish a whole religion based on one individual to
further your site.

I hope I don't have to take this further.

Thank you for your compliance.

Yours faithfully

Shak H.
-----------

I haven't (yet) removed or renamed the game, but have let Simon "Chaosmongers"
Ullyatt know of the complaint.

I'd be interested to hear your thoughts.  You can see the game here:
http://www.lofi-gaming.org.uk/speccy/cgc2005/ (it's game 07).

For the record, my reply was:

-----------
The game was not written by me, but by someone who entered this year's
competition.  As far as I can tell, the game is called Manic Muslim as a play
on words on the game "Manic Miner" - a game similar to this one.  It doesn't
have anything to do with Muslims in general, only one in particular.

The competition's other titles also include the UK's political parties,
teenagers, the Spanish, and the Second World War.  Titles from previous years
have been of similar content.  None of which are meant to be taken seriously,
and certainly none are intended to be disrespect any particular religion,
group of people, or social standing - certainly no more than any TV programme
such as Have I Got News For You, Spitting Image, or Brasseye.

I'm sorry you have seen the game to be offensive, but none of the games there
are my responsibility - I merely upload those games entered into the
competition as they are.  There are no rules regarding content, so
responsibility towards that lies with the author.  I do not believe in
censoring these games.  As for "furthering my site", I am simply hosting the
competition this year.  Previous years have been hosted by other people on
their sites.  I can provide links if you wish.

I will, however, pass your comments on to the author.

I hope you see my side of this,
Andy
---------

Also of note, is "Shak H" appears to share his email address with someone
called "John Smith", according to Google.
