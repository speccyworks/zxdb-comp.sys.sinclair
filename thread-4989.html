1995-11-29 14:27:21 Barry Plewa ZXDB: Emulate
Issue 3 of the Speccy Magazine, Emulate is now available!
This issue contains:

Complete Guide to Magic Knight (including playing guides)
Part 1 of the Spectrum Books Database
Part 3 of A-Z or Spectrum games and the Spectrum History
Games Charts - plus "What games make us jump?"
Playing tips - Everyones a Wally solution, plus how to find a hidden 
droid in Rebelstar!!
Classic reviews of JSW and Head over Heels
New Reviews of Z80, Alien and Speccy Senstaions 2 CD.
Plus all the usual features!

This months snapshot pack contains:
HERBERTS DUMMY RUN - Part 3 of the Wally adventure series
DEATH STALKER - Codemasters RPG-Arcade game
GRAHAM GOOCH'S CRICKET - For all you sports fans.
ROBOCOP - The smash hit game based on the movie (128k only)
FLYING FORMULA - Rare game, originally featured on ZX Computing.
DEMON FROM THE DARKSIDE - Part one of this adventure series.

PLUS Two EXCLUSIVE snaps:
JSW EDITOR 2 - An *excellent*, easy to use,JSW editor
JSW - The Continuing Adventures - Following on from the popular "Join 
the Jet Set" featured in Issue 2 - another exclusive JSW game to tackle!

The issue (text and snaps) will be available at both NVG and DEMON's 
FTP sites from 1st December at:
FTP.NVG.UNIT.NO/pub/sinclair/incoming
FTP.DEMON.CO.UK/pub/emulators/spectrum/magazines/emulate

The files are : EMULATE3.ZIP (Magazine) EMULSNA3.ZIP (snapshots)

Also, don't forget the EMULATE WEB PAGE!
To see the latest HTML version of Emulate, head to:
HTML://www.cs.bham.ac.uk/~dmb/speccy/emulate/index.html
It is excellent!!

Look out for an EMULATE SPECIAL - Coming soon!
