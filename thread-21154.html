Announcing...

LEN'S CHUNTEY CHALLENGE!

A great(crap) new game from the maker of CSSCGC2K entry "Apocalyptic
Asteroid Bombardment Death II : Cosmic Radiation"!

Will you be able to keep your Speccy's chuntey stable enough to load one of
your favourite games?
Or will it be disrupted by your mum walking into the room?
And is the room temperature okay?
Are you sitting at the right angle?
Will you say the magic "please load" incantation at the right time?
How many animals will you sacrifice?

Simulate the nervous tension of those bygone days when you had to indulge in
all manner of bizarre rituals to coerce the game into loading!

Will you stabilize the chuntey and load your game? There's only one way to
tell - play

            LEN'S CHUNTEY CHALLENGE!

Available for download exclusively ('cos it was too late to be entered into
CSSCGC2K) from the much-maligned and much-unavailable
alt.binaries.comp.sinclair newsgroup.

Len
