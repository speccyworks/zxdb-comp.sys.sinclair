Hello All,

As requested. A walkthrough of ADVENTURE 200 for
ZX-81 16k and ZX-Spectrum. Released in 1982 by
Foilkade Software / written by C.J.Coombs.

We desperately need help completing the final bit,
getting the MACE (a treasure item) past the troll.

The game is best played using the spectrum emulator.
We use the zx32 emulator - our saved games are for
that emulator :-)

You can download the game from World of Spectrum:-

http://www.worldofspectrum.org/infoseek.cgi?regexp=^Adventure+200$&pub=^C%2eJ%2e+Coombs$

We have (hand written) maps and saved games at certain
points (see walkthrough below). Anyone the posts a request
here can request them. I don't want to "pollute" a text
only group with .jpgs and binaries :-)

Please try and help us complete this game! We've tried
on-and-off since 1983 !!!

Patrick/

Version 1.0: 20th Jan. 2003.


Objective: Get all the treasures and get back to
the Palace.

N,S,E,W,NE,SE,SW,NW,U[P] and D[OWN].

End of phase 1. We have a saved game
at this point. Post a request for it.

Please note: We haven't used the
GRAIN with the CAGE to get the BIRD.
The BIRD is used to navigate the firedamp
mine - the bird stops singing when you
are 1 move from death. The bird enables
you to find the machine with the WRENCH
in it. TAKE WRENCH and the machine goes
from "not running" to "running". Now
you can goto all areas of the mine. Later
on we need to THROW WRENCH to stop the
machine and escape the mace guard...
--

Phase 3
=======

All hell is going to break loose now.
We need to leg it back to the firedamp
mine and put the wrench back to stop
the machine and cause an explosion that
kills the mace guard...


-- 

N N S E W N W W W W D W UP UP UP
E E D D NE UP THROW_WRENCH D W UP
UP W W UP

-- 
huge explosion, the mine is blocked by
rubble now and impassable - but the mace
guard troubles us no more.
The next part is getting the mace out of
the underground section. Another maze dodge
is required to get us out and avoid the
guard in the tower from killing us....
-- 

W W E N N E E S UP W N W W THROW_LAMP
W W

-- 
more hassle as the guards follow us outside.
throwing the lamp on the wooden bridge
"the bridge burns", and we're safe.

unfortunately this is as far as we can go
If you go one more move WEST, the troll
kills you. Drop MACE and you can approach
the troll. To get past the troll you
must DROP JUG. It breaks and you can pick
up CRYSTALS. THROW CRYSTALS and the troll
runs away. But how do you get the MACE
past him?
