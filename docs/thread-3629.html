A cheat for Manic Miner is :
   poke 35136,0  .. then you will never lost one of your holy miners.

   poke 34253,0   
   poke 34254,0
   poke 34255,0
   poke 33799,(cavern-1)   .. this will start Willy in any cavern.

It is tested on the Bug-Byte version of Manic Miner, perhaps it works also
on the Software Projects version.

But remember, the real challenge for Miner Willy isn´t consuming those
key-stuffs in the warehouse, but keeping cool in the heat of the solar
generator.
