Compared to the dreamcast, its amazing, the load times are minimal, in fact 
the only time you notice them, is if you want to skip the cinematic between 
levels on certain titles, and you find that you have to wait about 0.5 
seconds into the cine before you can skip, good way of hiding load time, 
and short enough anyway to not be too annoying.

I can't compare it to the PS2 or Xbox, because I have neither, and plan to 
get neither.

Rogue Leader is simply amazing, screen shots don't do it justice, well, 
they do, they show you how many polygons its pushing (about 15 million a 
second), but don't show the fact that the frame rate is usually smooth, the 
lighting is amazing, etc. The moment of jaw dropping has to be when you 
first hit the death star level, and realise that when you get close to the 
surface of the DS, all of the surface is true polygons. And all those 
hundreds of lasers^H^H^H^H^H^Hblasters being fired around you are providing 
dynamic lighting. Then later on when you're playing on Hoth, you get close 
to the ground, get so amazed by the fact that the hundreds of snow troopers 
are beautifully detailed and animated, that you slam straight into the leg 
of an AT-AT.

Oh, and a Tatooine dual-sunset has to be experienced to be believed (just 
play the training level at around sunset time real time).

The most bizarre part of Rogue Leader is the surround sound... Its supposed 
to be Dolby Pro Logic II, which needs a new receiver that supports PL2... 
yet my 3 year old (nearly) Yamaha receiver somehow manages to decode to a 
5.1 positional audio, at least the 'Surround test' produces a tie fighter 
that can be distinctly heard moving thru all 5 speakers even the 2 rear 
speakers. My only conclusion is that Yamaha implemented PL2 early and 
unofficially (Nowhere does it claim PL2 support, and the mode its in when 
Rogue Leader starts up is 'Pro Logic Enhanced' - perhaps Yamaha's DSP 
enhancement is what became PL2)

All that said, I haven't got to the most impressive part of RL yet, which 
is allegedly the level where you have to take on Star Destroyers.
