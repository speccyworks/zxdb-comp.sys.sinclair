Friday 25 July 2003

A busy month - reproductions, maps, lots of POKEs and a new section on
Tipshop

* Just to be perverse, I'll tell you about the new section on Tipshop
first.

We've had quite a few emails from people asking for help with games.
Some we were able to answer, some not. So, after some thought (and
writing a VB app to make it a little easier to maintain!), we've
launched the HAYLP section. As you might have gathered by now, it's a
section where you can have your game problems posted for others to see,
and help with. Eh? Dr Berkmann? Who he? (cough). All I ask is that
if you're submitting a problem, that you include a VALID email address.
This address will NEVER be posted on Tipshop, but I'll forward any
replies onto you myself. The solutions will then be added to Tipshop's
main database, and your plea removed. Sounds good, eh? Only if people
contribute....

* We had been sent emails asking for help on Adventure games we had
nothing for. Fortunately, the ever-helpful Dorothy Millard was able to
provide the goods from her own website,
http://members.optusnet.com.au/dmillard/index.htm. So there are now new
solutions to Woods Of Winter, Manor Of Doom, The and Going Critical. Our
sincere thanks, as always, go to Dorothy for her superb help.
