A BIG update, reproductions, HAYLP replies and an oops-I-should-have-
done-this-ages-ago!

* I may have mentioned to some of you that I was working on a BIG
Tipshop update. Well, I (in my opinion) wasn't kidding. Thanks to my
partner in crime, Nick Humphries OCRing about 50 issues of the YS
Adventures column, I was able to do the rest and I'm now VERY pleased
(and relieved!) to present pretty much every single hint, tip, bug
report, cheat and solution published in Your Sinclair's Adventures
section. There are 743 new entries from this.. I'm not going to list
them all :-) If anyone notices anything that's been missed, let us
know!

* Paul Equinox Collins granted us permission to reproduce his tips and
maps page for XOR. Our thanks to him for allowing us to do this.

* Corrected the link in the Credits section for Jim Grimwood's 
homepage (sorry!)

* Alex Waddington sent us scans of a newsletter containing tips for
Sorderon's Shadow. I OCRd them to make life easier for everyone, but
I've also included a link to the original scans.

* Pete Carass sent us some hints and a bug (that could be used as a
cheat) he found for Laser Squad

* A little bit of maintenance - Conquestador is just an english 
translation of Tres Luces De Glaurung, Las, so I've modified the
entries for both games to suit.

* Colin Attle became the first person to reply to someone needing
assistance in our HAYLP section. Bravo, sir!
