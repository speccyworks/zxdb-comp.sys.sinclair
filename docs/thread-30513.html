2004-12-01 12:29:25 Spirantho 
Hi everybody...

While procrastinating the other day I wondered about doing something
nice and perverse. Then I thought about doing something silly with a
ZX81, and finally an idea came to me...

The only thing 3D Monster Maze is really missing, I feel, is sound, so
I was wondering if anyone has hacked the 3DMM code to support sound
boxes like the ZonX-81 (an AY-3-8912 for those who aren't aware - very
funky but makes the poor '81 get a little hot under the collar).

If not, is the source available or is it time to dig out the
disassembler? I figure a 32K ZX81 would have plenty of space for adding
footsteps sounds (getting louder as Rex approaches), and maybe even
using a different channel for wizzy noises as you get closer to the
portal. Maybe even a soundtrack for the title screen, and a heartbeat
noise as you wander around the maze (we do have 3 channels + 1 noise
channel to play with).

Of course this would all be made a load easier if we have an emulator
which supports the ZonX! Does one exist? Is there a ZX81 emulator
author out there who can provide this? Or am I just insane to think
about it?

Answers on a postcard please...

2004-12-01 18:24:46 Mike Wynne Re: 3D Monster Maze and ZonX-81 - and emulators?
Brian.

Although the Sinclair 16k RAM Pack wasn't completely decoded and had 
shadows across memory space, other manufacturers didn't suffer this 
problem.  The ZX81 is capable of directly addressing 56k of RAM 
without resorting to any form of paging.
