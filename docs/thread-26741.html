Finally its done:

We are proud to announce a VGA-Box for the Spectrum. This box should be
connected to the VIDEO-Out of the Spectrum (RGB-Port of 128k/+2, or take the
signal from the modulator on the other machines, e.g. 48k or +2A/+3) and to
your nomal PC-VGA-Monitor.

We know that these devices are already on the market for years, but there
was none with a clear Spectrum picture and for a reasonable price. The price
of our box is 49,00 Euro, about 30 UKP, which is the cheapest around. The
box got switches for BRIGHT, COLOUR, CONTRAST and is therefore perfect for
using with the Spectrum.
At the moment it comes with a Power Supply and German plug, so you need an
Adapter (get it from Maplin or any other electronic store) for using it in
Britain (no problems in Spain, France, Italy, Netherlands, Czech
Republic...everywhere where you got round connectors to the mains). If there
is a big demand, we can of course also get Power supplies for UK-Plugs as
well.

Postage is:
UK, Italy: 10 Euro = 7 UKP
France, Netherlands, Belgie: 6 Euro
Germany: 4 Euro
Spain, Finnland: 17 Euro

Just write now in, if you are interested to order this item, so we know the
quantity. At the moment we have only 1 ready and thatï¿½s mine. We need to
know how many more we need to please write us.
