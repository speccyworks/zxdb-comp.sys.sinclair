According to Crash (Jun.1987 & Dec.1988) and the Your Sinclair Smash Tips
supplement (Aug.1988):
	POKE 35453,0	(8A7D)
will give you infinite lives.

In the unlikely event that you're playing from an original tape, then
according to Dave Leander in Your Spectrum (Sep.1985), enter and run:
	10 FOR A = 23296 TO 23309: READ Z: POKE A,Z: NEXT A
	20 DATA 55,62,255,221,33,0,64,17,0,192,205,86,5,201
Position the tape past the loader, and past the header on the main section,
then enter:
	RAND USR 23296
and play the tape. When it has loaded type:
	POKE 35453,0: RANDOMIZE USR 24770
and that should give you infinite lives as well.

definitely know allows you to enter POKEs. Don't know about Z80 (which is

If you don't like those emulators (and I can't think why not, as they're
bloomin' good), then you can always just load the game in, POKE it and save
it off again and play it on your favourite emulator (whatever that may be).

If you use an emulator on the Mac (which I don't think has a POKE ability,
but I could be horribly wrong as I haven't used it in ages), then you could
always use a hex editor. However, to do that you would need to know which
series of bytes to look for - which you would need to ask someone here for.
You would then search for the bytes, and change the appropriate number to
whatever new number, save it off and then load it back to your emulator...
