That's probably the one I sent you, then - but that's supposed to give a
complete solution to all the problems, so why haven't you finished it yet
after all this time? There are numerous other tips in the mags, so if I
knew what the sticking point was I could check them; although I'd expect
that they're just likely to cover selections of the same problems covered
in the YS solution, and some/all of them may already be available at The
Tipshop (http://www.the-tipshop.co.uk/). If they're not there and you're
still stuck, and can identify some particular problems, then I'll check
the other back issues.

Here's the list of tip entries, in case you have any of the mags:

  S YS  9.87  p. 86 .SH1  solution first half
  S YS 10.87  p. 89 .SH2  solution second half
  T C   1.88  p. 58
  T SU  2.87  p. 79
