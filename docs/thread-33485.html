* Archie Robins sent in new POKEs for Black Magic, Dynamite Dux,
I.C.U.P.S., Last Mohican, Macadam Bumper, Night Shift and Tomahawk, as
well as corrections for assorted others.

* Richard Hern sent in an addition to his tips for Insector Hecti in
the Interchange which were first printed in Crash issue 93. That's only
a gap of 16 years!

* Rob Livsey sent in some interesting hacks for Oracle's Cave, The.

* Skarpo sent in tips for Thor and tips/solution for Blip Blop,
dedicated
to the memory of our good friend Viktor Drozd.

* The enigmatic Stu sent in an apparently never-before published cheat
for Cabal.

* Colin Attle (AKA Ladderman) sent in maps for Moto Cross Simulator ,
Mr. Heli , Nemesis the Warlock , Night Breed , Overkill and Psycho
Soldier.

* Added RZX Archive links for 19 Part 1: Boot Camp, Buffalo Bill's Wild
West Show, Perico Delgado Maillot Amarillo, Tour 91 and Train: Escape
to Normandy, The.

* Added maps for Aaargh! , Fall Guy, The , Izzy Wizzy , Phantomasa 2 ,
Sootland , Strangeloop , Vampire's Empire , View to a Kill, A and
Vindicator!.
