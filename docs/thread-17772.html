Hi,

I'm trying to track down, which 8-bit publication, produced a "Book of Maps"
as a supplement one month. I remember the Book of Maps was about A5 in size,
and contained a compilation of maps for different popular games of the time.

The map that I remember is the Nodes of Yesod Map. I do realise that this
was published in some of the Sinclair mags also, but am keen to find who
published the Book of Maps. Seem to think it was a multi-format magazine
such as Your Computer or C&VG, but just can't find any reference to it in
the issues I have.

Any help would be greatly appreciated, as this has been bugging me and a
friend for about 14 years now!

Cheers

Ian

Sinclair User published an A5 "Good Map Guide" booklet with the March 1987
issue, but it didn't include a map for Nodes of Yesod. There were a few
pull-out map supplements in the mags, but the only Nodes map in the SPOT
indexed titles was in Crash (Oct.1985).

Your recollection that it was in one of the multi-format mags will most
probably be right, then, as I never bought them at the time, so they're
not indexed on SPOT. The only Sinclair-specific title which might have
published such a booklet is ZX Computing, which was still around up until
mid-1987 (Nodes was released in Aug.1985) - I don't yet have the ZXC
issues covering that period. So now at least you know where not to look!
