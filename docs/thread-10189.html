Hi everyone ! 

As you might have heard I am off to do my army service for this country :( It
starts on 23th of September and will last for 6 or 7 months (depends on how they
decide). I will probably be without internet access during this time and won't
be able to either check my email, update the ZXTape homepage or utilities. Also
the e-mail address might be down for the duration of this, so please don't send
any emails during this time... when I am back I will contact everyone :)

I will do the last ZXTape homepage update this week, hopefully they will reboot
the machine one day, otherwise I will ask someone to do that for me :) 

Also the new release of ZXTape format will happen on wednesday or around then
with some BIG changes (hopefully making it better ;) ).

If you make some great TZX files in the meantime and would like to share them
with others then please upload them to NVG archive (ftp.nvg.unit.no
/pub/sinclair/incoming).

Martijn v.d. Heide generously offered to help anyone that will have any problems
with TZX files or with the format during the time I am digging the trenches :)
... his email is …@… (thanx a lot !!!)

What more can I say ? Well... have a good time (I hope I will too) and keep the
Speccy alive ! :)
