2001-12-02 22:29:27 Gareth Brisbane ZXDB: The Ice Temple ZXDB: Firelord
As indeed suggested - firelord is completed (nearly) when the 4 charms are
traded for the firestone. When handed to the dragon (who I think looks more
like a unicorn), you gain the rank of Overlord.
If the charms are use to steal the firestone (I slowed the emulator down -
who knows how hard it is in real time), then the evil queen remains trapped
and you gain the rank of Firelord.

I couldn't get 100% of the map visited, there are two squares which are
inaccessible - one on the 2nd row, far right (which appears to be the left
hand most square on the third row - where the witch is as it is outside the
rectangle map) and one not far from the start which has no entrances. There
are no hidden teleport codes after testing using brute force.

You can, however, get to the square to the east of the life/life magic place
which appears to be blocked by fire, using the following technique. Go to
the princess, activate the fire release, open the portcullis and teleport to
life/life. When you arrive, the fire will now be sporadic instead of
permanent. The square to the right contains a reeve which sells lives at the
cost of two items.... who would! It also contains a fire release to let you
back through the fire.

I have a hand drawn map which I will provide if anyone is interested.

At this point I am also mapping the incredibly boring game of the ice
temple. The fun starts to vanish because it is quite monotonous. Again, hand
drawn - if anyone knows a good tool for a 2-d map - I'm happy to see it. If
I finish - I'll let you know (probably more for the sake of dejanews).

Gareth
