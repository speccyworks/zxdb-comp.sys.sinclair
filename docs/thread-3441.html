They allow you to skip between levels - the level you get to is determined
by holding down the number keys.
In Bug Byte version, hold down 6 + combination of 1-5 for room required.
To get right combination, think of level code in binary, reverse it, and
press keys to set bits. ie

6                      =     Central Cavern.
6 + 1               =    Cold Store
6 + 2                =     Menagerie
6 + 1+2           =  Abandoned Uranium workings.
etc.

For infinite lives, you merge the loader, and add the line:
25 POKE 35136,0

All this is from memory, so the details may be slightly out, however I'm
sure someone will correct them if they are.

6031769 - Which is in the FAQ, the group charter and many other 
places...

Oh, and press 6 and a combination of the keys 1-5 to jump to a 
particular room. Some interesting rooms are:

126 - Abandoned Uranium Workings.
  Easiest level in the game. Good for practice.
36 - Eugene's Lair.
  Funniest level in the game. See if you can work out the connection 
  between a load of flapping toilets and a certain Mr E Evans.
136 - Processing Plant.
  Staffed by Pac Man lookalikes.
1236- Miner Willy Meets The Kong Beast.
  Eat your heart out Nintendo. :-)
246 - Attack Of The Mutant Telephones.
  Anyone else remember Jeff Minter? I do!
1346 - Skylab Landing Bay.
  Memorising the patterns of the falling Skylabs is the key to this 
  one.
56 - The Warehouse.
  Hardest level in the game. Jump, dig, jump, splat! Good fun when 
  you finally finish it.
256 - The Solar Power Generator.
  Most nailbiting level in the game, and the only one where you 
  actually have to keep a watch on the air supply.
1256 - The Final Challenge.
  Last level, but rather boring.
