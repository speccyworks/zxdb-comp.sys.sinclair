Emulate! issue dates (entries 42459–42463). ZXDB dates issues 2–6 to the last day of a month, which looks like a guess. The group shows when each came out:
#2: 1995-10-15, not 1995-09-30 (1995-10-15);
#3: 1995-11-29, not 1995-10-31 (1995-11-29);
#4: on the web 1996-03-08, not 1995-11-30 (1996-03-08);
#5: 1996-05-04, not 1996-02-29 (1996-05-04);
#6: 1996-08-18, not 1996-05-31 (1996-08-18).

#1 (1995-08-31) agrees. Emulate! Special Edition No.1 (1995-11-30, 1995-11-30) seems to have no entry.

ZXZVM (21817): ZXDB says 1999. The first public release was 1998-03-23 (1998-03-23) and v1.00 was 1998-07-06 (1998-07-06). See the versions table.
The Time Hole (10554): ZXDB says 1999, but the game first came out on 2000-06-06.
Avalanche (31914) and Mayhem (31948), ZX81 games by Revival Studios: ZXDB says 2011. The group says the full release was at the Spectrum 30 event, 8–9 September 2012 (2012-09-05). Mayhem's Videopac version from 2011 may be the source of that year.
BBC BASIC (37271, J.G. Harston): ZXDB says 2005, but it was on mdfs.net and working by 2003-11-12 (2003-11-12).
Chopper Drop 3000 (32008): ZXDB's 2009 and its two files are the compiled re-release (2009-05-05). The game first came out in BASIC, in English and French, on 2008-01-06 (2008-01-06).
Subliminal Extacy 3 (28096): ZXDB says 2001-04-30. The group shows it available on 2001-04-27 (2001-04-27).
Jet Set Willy: The '96 Remix (16711): ZXDB says 1996, probably when it was written. It was first made public on 1999-10-04.
Tunnel Escape 2 is in ZXDB twice, as 30737 (in the CSSCGC 2001 tag; no date of its own, 2001 from the CSSCGC 2001 compilation) and 17815 Tunnel Esc. 2 (2001-11-09, Minigame Collection). The group shows one game, released 2001-04-23.
C68 (42380, the QL C compiler): ZXDB names Tim Swenson as author. He wrote about C68; the group credits the compiler to others (releases by Philippe Troin, runtime disks by Thierry Godefroy).
Shadow of the Unicorn (4000066, Timex 2068, 1985, no author): its file ShadowOfTheUnicorn.dck.zip may be Andrew Owen's 2009 LROS cartridge version (2009-02-08), filed under the original game.
