2007-01-08 10:00:32 Matt Rudge CSSCGC Crap Games Contest 2006
In a dramatic break with tradition, I've actually accomplished
something on time!  So the voting for the CGC2006 is now up and running.

Just as a reminder folks, we want to find the *crappest* (or, "most
crap" for those of you who have a Henry Higgins approach to English)
game of the lot.  So if a game is particularly crap, then give it 10
out of 10 for "crap factor".  If it's actually quite good, then
obviously it would score low on the "crap factor" scale.

The voting is open until the 12th February, because I'm heading off for
a couple of weeks at the end of the month.  Afterwards, I'll tot up the
scores and arrange for Lister to host the awards ceremony[1]

If that all sounds simple enough, then start breaking my PHP scripts...
now!

[1] What?  Do you think I'm made of money?
