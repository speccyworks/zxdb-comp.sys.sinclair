According to the solution in Sinclair User 103 (Sep.1990) the key to the
Spirit's Abode (which it numbers 4) is in the Hot Baths room. It says:
"A little awkward to find, you must fall down the hole in the bath after
draining it by pulling level [sic]. Now, stand on the highest step and
look up. When you see a little hole, action it."

Flaming Hell. No wonder you couldn't find it in eight years.

The Sinclair User solution is only about a quarter the length of the Your
Sinclair solution, although the SU one says it's "for the Prince version
of the game". Oh, hang on, the YS one also says "that was the solution
assuming you took the part of the Prince". Hmmm - stra-a-ange ... In case
you're a girly, JH, the YS one also includes the Princess variations, so
I'll send you a scan of that one shortly.
