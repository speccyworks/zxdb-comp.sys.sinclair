From a quick read of the help file, it probably never will.
Seems the author regards suggestions from people as all being
'stupid' and that bug reports should be so detailed that only
another emulator author or Z80 hacker could really help out.
Case in point - he complains about having to add a POKE feature
and sneers about it. You wonder why he bothered releasing it if
he didn't want people to suggest improvements!

Reading further, you find that he's pissed off that his own ZXS
snapshot format hasn't taken off, and he's been forced to
support the SLT format (or "horse designed by committee" as he
calls it). One does wonder how the hell he expects anyone to
support his format when ZX32 has only in the last month been
widely available... when he doesn't give out an email address so
people can talk to him about it... when he doesn't even post
here so misses out on 90% of the Sinclair scene on the net...
he does appear to be aware of this newsgroup, as he mentions it
in the help file. He also certainly reads the FAQ.

Contrast his "attempts to popularize" ZXS with the SLT design,
which was a cooperation between myself and several emulator
authors, or the TZX design which was a cooperation again, and
carried out openly on this very ng.

Futhermore SLT was designed for an initial specific purpose
(i.e. cutting down the amount of files scattered about for
level-loading games) and expanded to cater for additional
information such as loading screens. Comparing it with a
"do-everything" format like ZXS is pointless. Putting down the
FAQ for including details of the SLT spec but not the ZIP spec
also displays a certain ignorance of what the Speccy FAQ is
meant to cover! I would count support for opening ZIPs in an
emulator as a non-feature - not something worth bothering with.
With programs like ZipFolders around, what's the point?

Had anyone here even heard about Vaggelis' ZXS format before
they read the ZX32 help file? So what kind of "attempt to
popularize" it was that then?? Not forgetting that 'ZXS' is a
name already used by another type of file - i.e. the tape files
at the Russian Sinclair museum site. And 'ZXT' as a suggestion
for ZXS files with tape data conflicts with Tomaz's ZXTape
format!

Vaggelis - if you're reading this, don't give up now. We have
only recently found out about your emulator, it's superb, and
with a little more work and cooperation with the Sinclair
community, it could beat even Z80. It seems such a shame to stop
development now, and leave us with yet another dead-end emulator
that goes nowhere and gets forgotten.
