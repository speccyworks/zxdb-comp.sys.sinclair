Hi Jeff,

> Good stuff! Is there a version of PlayTZX that will dump C64 and Amstrad
> games back out to tape? Cos I have both machines and very little in the
> way of software.

You can download Tomaz's 64Utils05.zip from CIA/Lemon and other C64 sites.
There's a util in there called 64TAPVOC which has the option to replay a TAP file
to a VOC or the SB output.  In addition PlayTZX will play C64 TZXs anyway.

I think there's a util called pTAP which will save TAPs back to the C64
Datacassette
in a similar way to making the TAP (with mTAP) in the first place.  Never really
been
interested yet, I'm just busy preserving them.

However, hold fire on using the 64Utils05, because they only handle v0 TAPs at
present.
We need to use v1 TAPs otherwise the pause info will be wrong.
No offence Tomaz :-)

Oh Amstrad tapes can probably be saves straight back onto tape as you do for the
Spectrum.

At the end of the day, all the dumps are as good or better than the originals and
can be dumped
back to tape with very a good signal strength.  Apart from C64 tapes, they might
work, but maybe
not.  C64 TAPs are bascially like the TZX DRB block or a CSW file.  I think CSW it
probably
better ?  When the 64Utils are fixed for V1 TAPs it might be highly acceptable to
then use a
standard cassette deck and CSW to get a CSW/WAV sample for which you can make a
TZX.
It might also be possible to combine the sources of all the 3 major formats and
make a MakeTZX
type engine to make a TZX/CDT/64T? on the fly.  Someone probably need to make a
W32 interface
for this.  Maybe someone may like to refine STL (Spectrum Tape Loader) I dunno ?
