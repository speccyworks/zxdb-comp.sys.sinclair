*        EMULATE! ISSUE 1   -  Out Now!!        *
*                                               *
*************************************************

Yes, at last after weeks of work, EMULATE! No.1 is ready for
release. This is a disk magazine covering all aspects of Spectrum
Emulation. It is in plain text format so is readable by all 
computers.

No.1 features articles on History of Spectrum, A-Z of POKES, a massive
article on Dizzy (including solutions for all the games), the complete
REBELSTAR reviews, A-Z of Spectrum Games Part 1....plus loads more.

I will be releasing two parts of the magazine:
TEXT ONLY - The text only mag is 150k (About 50k when zipped)
SNAPSHOTS - All the snapshots mentioned in the main mag (About 400k)
			
The snapshots included with Issue 1 are TWICE SHY, WAR IN MIDDLE EARTH (The
English edition), 16/48 ISSUE 20 (TAP only), PYJAMARAMA, SPLIT PERSONALITIES,
JET SET WILLY 3, plus EDITORS for JET SET WILLY and CHAOS.
Also included are GIFs of some loading screens for the above, as well as 
some end screens for certain games.

I will send the text mag directly to anyone who sends me a personal 
request, but the snapshots version will only be available from
the FTP sites. (due to it's size.)

I will make it available on the main FTP sites from the 1st September.
Archive names: EMULATE1.ZIP (Text mag)    EMULSNA1.ZIP (Text and snaps)

Any feedback and articles for future issues would be greatly appreciated.

Finally thanks to all those who helped out on the first issue.
If I get a positive enough response from this issue I will be sure to 
produce more....!

Thanks,

--
