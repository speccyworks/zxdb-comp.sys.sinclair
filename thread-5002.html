EMULATE! Special Edition No.1 Out Now!

2 posts, 1995-11-30 – 1995-12-04

1995-11-30 15:35:29 Barry Plewa 
Hot on the heels of Issue 3 comes the first Special Edition of Emulate!

Thanks to Ben Bayliss's excellent re-production of the YS Smash Tips 
supplement on his web page, I have transferred the whole supplement 
over to plain ASCII text and released it as this special edition.

The whole mag contains hundreds of cheats, pokes and solutions, 
amounting to over 350k of pure games-tips!!

This excellent collection includes all the speedlock and bleeploaders 
for hackers out there, plus complete walk-throughs of games.

Sure to keep you from getting frustrated with your favourite Speccy 
game over Christmas!!

You can find the magazine soon at both Demon and NVG hopefully, in the 
usual emulate directory. The file is called EMUSPEC1.ZIP!! 

Thanks to Ben Bayliss for letting me use his web pages in this magazine 
and also to Damion Yates who OCR'ed the original.
