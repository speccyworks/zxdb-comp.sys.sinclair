Roger Kean was a terrific writer. His magazine was very much at the
heart of Speccy gaming and gave it the profile and respect that it was
due. I get sick and tired of people slagging off Crash in its early
days, pointing to how naff it was, but lest we forget that back in
1984 YS was a fairly po-faced technically-minded rag and Sinclair User
was obsessed with telling you how the dK'Tronics keyboard was now
tuppence cheaper. Only Crash realised that the majority of the
Speccy's uder base cared more about Matthew Smith than Clive Sinclair
and wanted to get a bit of buying guidance. Roger Kean was at the
heart of this, wrote the bulk of the magazine himself and we all owe
him a lot of thanks.
