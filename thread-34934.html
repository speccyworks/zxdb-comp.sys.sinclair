Having secured permission to distribute the TS1000 / ZX81 improved ROM
under the GPL a while back I thought I really should put up a binary
and source code. I spent the weekend getting the source to compile and
it now produces an identical binary to the original (improved) ROM.
The source isn't commented (Logan and O'Hara do a better job than I
could) but I've used sensible labels in place of all the 'magic
numbers'. It should make it a lot easier to follow. Here it is:

https://sites.google.com/site/open81rom/

Cheers!

-Andrew

my current toying & fooling with asm is centered on firmware monitor 
development ;)

ZX80 & 81 has a rather unconventional screen handling, here the main 
difficulty in development of monitor firmware, at least for me. (I 
suspect that a D command can be an ugly beast because of the "dynamic 
screen memory" whose sooner or later collide with E-LINE)

ZX80 & 81 at their times was most economical & readily available micro, 
and even available in kit form, so they was perfect for control & 
industrial systems & like apps, and was feasible replacing the ROM with 
an EPROM with the appropriate firmware, and perhaps also a monitor for 
on-field diagnostic & setup...

The same apply for the spectrum, for sure I knew of a field use of the 
speccy by amateur astronomers as calculator for pointing the scopes & 
other on-the spot calculations (all needed support was a 4" 5" B/W TV 
and car batteries or even the car itself, with the engine in idle 
(fitting for a UK comp, at least once the car involved was a Morris Mini...)

side question, no one has tried to rebuild the .pdf of the ZX81 service 
manual and assembler instructions, whose after a certain page is 
unreadable to the point to be useless ?
