Unfortunately, before the TZX format came along, we had several formats for
games. .SNA, .Z80, .TAP, .ZXS to name but a few. TZX is the format of
choice - it stores a dump of the original tape that the game came from,
including all multiload levels and loading routines (speeclock, bleepload
etc) which the other formats couldn't handle (with the exception of .TAP,
which is an obselete version of TZX). Of the others, I would recommend .Z80,
but even then these are just a dump of the Spectrum's memory, and are
usually inaccurate and missing some data necessary to run the game. It is to
be hoped that the TZX Vault's efforts will continue in procuring perfect
copies of the original tapes, because these will become the de-facto source
for spectrum games in the near future. As I have said elsewhere, TOSEC
should really only be used for obtaining games which are "Distribution
Denied" because any others are either

a) Available as guaranteed exact copies in the TZX Vault - something which
the TOSEC TZXs are not, and are for the most part "faked" or "cracked"
(laughable for a machine which had virtually no copy protection).

b) If they aren't available at the TZX Vault yet, then they probably will be
soon, and will be better copies than the TOSEC ones, due to that guarantee
of quality.

Until a game comes along as a perfect TZX, most people download the World Of
Spectrum's Z80 or SNA version. These versions are themselves replaced with
TZX when they become available though.

As for generating a signature, you'd be lucky to do that with the Z80 and
SNA files, as they were dumps of the games after the game had loaded, and so
their contents vary greatly as to the time they were snapped - they aren't
ROMS whose content never changes.

Hope that helps,
    D.

www.WorldOfSpectrum.Org which carry far more information that I can relay
