> If  someone hadc the tapes, how would they convert it?

1 tape playing device
1 audio connection lead thingy
1 computer of some description which has required port to connect
audio connection lead thingy to
1 adapter of unknown description in case the required computer
specification is not met
1 copy of maketzx or alternative software compiled for the operating
system installed on the designated computer

Insert one end of the audio connection lead thingy into the headphone
socket of the tape playing device, insert the other end into the
computer, utilising the optional adapter if required.  Stir gently
until combined.

Press the eject button on the tape playing device firmly until the
tape mechanism exits the tape playing device, leaving a gap into which
the 300ml of milk can be poured.  Add the tape, ensure the lid is
securely in place and blend.

Power on the computer and preheat to gas mark 4.  Once pre-heated,
load up the maketzx or equivalent, following the instructions on the
packet.  Squeeze the play button on the tape device once the software
is in record mode.

Add the cinnamon.

Leave for 30 minutes or until slightly browned on top.

This delicious ca... software is best served with a warm Spectrum and
a glass of wine.
