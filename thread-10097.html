On Sun, 14 Sep 1997 00:37:34 GMT, …@… (W R Bennett) wrote:

>How did you translate the Ogham scroll?  I pondered those cryptic texts 
>for _years_ before giving up in disgust.

I went to a library and looked up the meaning of the symbols. I've just
looked up my original notes for the game (still got them after 12 years!),
which don't make an awful lot of sense now, but they've reminded me that
the Ogham symbols in the game are incorrect, and translate as
"CROLLROADTEBB", which rather complicated matters; not knowing which
letters were right and which wrong I was rather stumped at that point. I
got the correct translation after writing to the 'Gordo Greatbelly'
adventure help at Sinclair User (my letter's in #43, Oct.1985). It turned
out that what it was supposed to have said was "CROSSROADTENN", which
gives the position of Calum's Hammer relative to the cross in Stormbase.


>And how was one supposed to join the two halves of the torc together?  
>And did it do anything?

The torc made Cuchulainn invisible so he could walk past the serpent
Nathair to get Dagda's Cauldron. I think there was an invisible back
entrance there so you could get the cauldron without passing the serpent,
so you didn't need the torc (this is effectively a cheat, as there's no
way you could know about the invisible door in normal play, without
passing Nathair first, for which you need the torc). You have to leave
that way, anyway, as the cauldron makes Cuchulainn visible again.

I can't remember how the halves were joined; maybe just holding them
together or dropping them together. Neither the Gargoyle hints sheet or
the Crash solution I have give any specific instructions on how the halves
of the torc are joined once found. I noticed a solution a while ago on SSD
(www.icemark.ch/zx/ssd); maybe that has more info (although it's most
likely the one from Crash). I definitely got them joined somehow, as I
remember Cuchulainn turning invisible.
