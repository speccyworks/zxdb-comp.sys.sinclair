Well folks, this is it!

My plea for more votes last week generated another two, so although this chart
isn't really a fair representation of popular opinion, I'm going to print it
anyway. So there. If you don't agree with this chart, then it's your own fault
for being lethargic.

Comp.Sys.Sinclair Crap Games Compilation - The Players' Chart
-------------------------------------------------------------

1. Dark Room Simulator
2. Naughts and Crosses
3. Valley Racer 3D
4. Purple Demon
5. Lego Simulator
6. SpecDaleks
7. Credit Calculator
8. Chess
9. Sokoban
10. Anthea Turner's National Lottery Simulator


So, Dark Room Simulator wins the accolade of the 'Player's favourite piece of
crap'! Not bad considering it arrived so late (and as an ASCII listing!) that it
almost didn't get included! Congrats to Iain Scrimgeour, it's author, and
congrats to me too 'cos I typed it in.
