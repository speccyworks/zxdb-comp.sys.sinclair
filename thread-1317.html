In '83/'84 I wrote a Spectrum conversion of the Williams coin op Robotron
for Atarisoft in the UK. Although I got paid for it, it was never
published thanks to Atarisoft going under.

I did give it out to a few people, and am hoping someone has a working
copy somewhere. It's a stunning conversion (even if I do say so myself!)
and I'd love to share it with the 'new' world of emulators. If anyone has
any information or can help in any way, please let me know.

Paul Holmes

[Wrote & cowrote: Bomb Jack, Bomb Jack 2, Wild West Hero, Dustman.
Designed & codesigned: Grand National, Frank Bruno's Boxing & others.]
