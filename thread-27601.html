       ADVENTURE 200 for ZX-81 & ZX-SPECTRUM
+--------------------------------------------------+
| Walkthrough by: Patrick Beilby & Derek Parkinson.|
+--------------------------------------------------+
Version 2.0: 8th April 2003.
Comment: FINALLY SOLVED - GOT MACE PAST THE TROLL :)


Objective: Get all the treasures and get back to
the Palace.

Treasures
=========

DIAMONDS
GOLD
MACE
SEAL
RING

Command set.
============
DE[scribe] <item>
DI[g]
DRI[nk]
DR[op <item>
LI[ght] <item>
LO[ck]
I[nventory]
Q[uit]
TA[ke] <item>
TH[row] <item>
UN[lock]
V[iew]

Movement commands:-
N,S,E,W,NE,SE,SW,NW,U[P] and D[OWN].


PHASE 1
=======

S S D E E TAKE_FISH N TAKE_LAMP
S W W UP N N E LIGHT_LAMP W S S
S D E E N N NE E S W E N N
TAKE_JUG S S E W S E NW SW E
DRINK TAKE_COIN TAKE_WATER W N UP W
N N E N TAKE_AXE S S E E THROW_COIN

E E S E E S E E D E E SE E

D W UP UP W W UP W W S S S S
W S E E UNLOCK TAKE_DIAMONDS LOCK

N THROW_WATER UP N TAKE_GRAIN S

N E UP W W N UP E N
TAKE_SPADE S W D THROW_WATER S
D N E E N SE E D D D SE E UP UP
E N DRINK TAKE_WATER

--
should be at EAST SIDE OF RIVER FORD
with:-
DIAMONDS,LAMP,AXE,FISH,SPADE
JUG,WATER,KEYS,WRENCH,GRAIN

End of phase 1.

Please note: We haven't used the
GRAIN with the CAGE to get the BIRD.
The BIRD is used to navigate the firedamp
mine - the bird stops singing when you
are 1 move from death. The bird enables
you to find the machine with the WRENCH
in it. TAKE WRENCH and the machine goes
from "not running" to "running". Now
you can goto all areas of the mine. Later
on we need to THROW WRENCH to stop the
machine and escape the mace guard...
--

PHASE 2
=======
N E E E E E E DRINK N DIG DRINK
TAKE_WATER S S D S UP NE UP Y
A B D G K N N W W N W S E S NE
UP TAKE_SEAL D E N E N S N S N[O]
D SW D N
UP DRINK N N DIG DRINK TAKE_WATER
S W W DRINK W W W S S W D
D W UP UP UP UP W W W W UP W W
NE N E S TAKE_FLOWER DIG TAKE_GARLIC
S W S W S E E D E E S W W THROW_GARLIC

--
the snake swallows everthing you throw at
it except garlic...he'll go away now.
You can go north here, but it seems there
is only a 1 room maze. i.e. drop something
and try to go N E or W. You are at the
same place. Go S get back to the high chamber.
We need to get some more garlic for the
next section, Castle Desmodus - to ward of
the vampire!
--

E E N W W UP W W NE N E S DIG TAKE_GARLIC
S W S W S E E D E E SE E D D
D SE E UP UP E N N N N UNLOCK
N UP E N NW W NW D S D TAKE_RING
UP N UP E E E S W D S S S S W NW

--
to avoid the castle dogs we need to dodge
into a maze and emerge back at the pothole
--

W W E E D D W UP UP UP UP W W S

--
should now be at 4 PASSAGES with:-
DIAMONDS LAMP AXE FISH FLOWER GARLIC
SPADE SEAL JUG RING KEYS WRENCH GRAIN

End of Phase 2.
--

PHASE 3
=======

W W S UP E E D E N TAKE_BRANDY
S W UP W W D N E E N SE S S
E UP LOCK UP E THROW_BRANDY
E N E W D W TAKE_GOLD E LOCK
SW S N NW D D N N E D
D D SE E UP E E S S E S SE S NE
NE TAKE_MACE
--
All hell is going to break loose now.
We need to leg it back to the firedamp
mine and put the wrench back to stop
the machine and cause an explosion that
kills the mace guard...
--

N N S E W N W W W W D W UP UP UP
E E D D NE UP THROW_WRENCH D W UP
UP W W UP

--
huge explosion, the mine is blocked by
rubble now and impassable - but the mace
guard troubles us no more.
The next part is getting the mace out of
the underground section. Another maze dodge
is required to get us out and avoid the
guard in the tower from killing us....
--

W W E N N E E S UP W W THROW_MACE

--
This was the last hurdle. You are at one
side of the dry canyon. The troll automatically
kills you if you carry the mace. You must
throw the mace across the canyon and collect
it on the other side. (at the location you
*originally* picked the JUG up!)
--

N W THROW_LAMP TAKE_LAMP W W W DROP_JUG
TAKE_CRYSTALS THROW_CRYSTALS W W W S S
D E E N N NE E S W E N N TAKE_MACE S S
E W S E NW SW N U W N W

--

COMPLETED! POUR YOURSELF A BEER... :)
