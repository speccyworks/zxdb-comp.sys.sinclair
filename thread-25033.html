2002-04-17 19:07:25 Dunny 
Hello all -

    I *know* I said that I'd try and get a release of SPIN out last weekend.
However, there's a nasty bug in the core (or the tzx renderer, however
unlikely) which prevents speedlock games from loading. This is a serious
setback, so if anyone's absolutely desperate, I'll try and get an alpha
release out very soon, in the next couple of days.

    That's that out of the way - now for the news...

    Mark Woodmass and I have decided to team up and make SPIN a joint
development. He's had so much input into SPIN already (I didn't have the
faintest clue how a z80 worked until he taught me, and my knowledge of Z80
and 8086 asm was non-existant) it makes sense that he should be one of the
official developers now. He's responsible for the Z80 core and the tzx
fastloaders at the moment - I'm going to be concentrating on the UI and when
it's up to scratch as an everyday common or garden emulator, I'll work on
the BASIC ide that I've wanted to do for so long.

    This leads me nicely to the next part - that Mark is no longer able to
maintain SpecEMU - the ideas that have come recently to light for his
emulator would require a large amount of work to implement, and would be
trivial in SPIN as it stands. This means that SpecEMU is no longer in
development, and is, as of today, unsupported. Any mail sent to SpecEMU's
author will effectively be replied to with a short "no longer supported".

    Any mail (for the time being) relating to SPIN can be sent directly to
me, or to …@… which will be checked regularly when SPIN is
released.

    God, I hope SPIN is worth it :)

    Thanks for your patience - hopefully this can be a very interesting
project.

    Paul Dunn.
