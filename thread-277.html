Believe me, I had many an amusing hour finding out all the stupid 
messages in the game.

For example, if you carry Elrond, every four moves or so, the following
message appears :

	Elrond gives some lunch to you
	Elrond is carrying to much

Alternatively, you can top Elrond with any object at your disposal

eg: KILL ELROND WITH LUNCH

	You attack Elrond with some lunch
	With one well placed blow your lunch cleaves his skull
	Elrond is dead

Of course, you can always attack something else with Dead Elrond now

And we all remember what happens when you drink the wine........

(if not, try "cat [filename] | sed 's/s/sh/g'" on any text file in UNIX to
 get some idea!)

Then there's the infamous DO command which does all sorts of strange things

Try SMASH GANDALF DO or KILL KEY DO or SMASH KEY DO for some odd happenings

However the most funniest bugged message I have seen appears on Lord Of
The Rings, and is a simple way of demonstrating how confused the parser
can get

Just type KILL

The effect something like this

	You attack you
	Your fist passes close to your ear
	Pippin attacks you
	Pippin brandishes his fists but you are on guard
	Sam attacks Pippin
	Sam gives Pippin a nasty slash in the ear

And so ad infinitum........
