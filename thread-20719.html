Tip: move up and down on the first line to make the
hangers come close to you, then jump. If you're lucky,
you'll find yourself in the middle of the screen, and
the hangers will never catch you! Reach for the coat
*BUT...* don't take it yet. Step up, down forth, back
until you're low on AIR or a hanger has come close, then
perform a professional coat getting (that is, get the coat).
Of course, with so little AIR in the higher levels, you'll
have to be fecking lucky and just go straight to the jacket,

Right, I've taken on board your ideas and tinkered with the game. Now
there is an air tank on each level, normally somewhere in the centre of
the screen to make it riskier to go for. At the end of each level
there's a points bonus for the air you have left too. To take the edge
off all these good points, the higher the level the smaller the
distance your jumps will take you. This way you won't be able to stock
up on air and lure the hatstands into a corner, then just jump next to
your coat. Hehehe!

It will make you use your jumps to reach items such as the rocket boots
or the air.
