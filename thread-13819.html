You could try sites like:
Spectrum Adventurer (http://home.virtual-pc.com/isblpx/index.html)
and
Sinclair Software Database (http://www.icemark.ch/zx/SSD-II)

Both NVG (ftp://ftp.nvg.unit.no/pub/sinclair)
 and WoS (ftp://ftp.gns.getronics.nl/pub/os/sinclair)
have some solutions as well.

Both these games are a bit obscure, so I wouldn't be too optimistic.

No solutions in the Speccy mags, no tips on Peter Pan at all, but these
following for Snow Queen in Crash:

[leaving the Magic Garden in Part One - Crash 38 (Mar.1987)]
""
First you must learn to talk to plants. You must find the right book and
read it twice. Once you have done this go and talk to the Michaelmas
Daisies outside the north gate. You will now need a key to the gate - this
can be obtained from the person who is frequently entering and leaving the
grounds.
""

[various - Crash 41 (Jun.1987)]
""
If you are having difficulty talking to the raven, show it the opal to
attract its attention.
If the city gates are closed when you arrive, go to the sheltered niche
with dry leaves and sleep.
If you have trouble with the big blind dog, just drop the mothballs as it
relies on smell.
""

Another long shot is to do a phrase search on AltaVista
(http://www.altavista.com). I did a phrase search on "Brian Howarth"
recently (the Mysterious Adventures author) and found all sorts of Speccy
adventure sites I'd never heard of before. Though with Peter Pan and The
Snow Queen both being based on classic works of fiction you're rather
likely to get a lot of non-Speccy matches. Maybe try "St Bride" rather
than "Snow Queen", for example.
