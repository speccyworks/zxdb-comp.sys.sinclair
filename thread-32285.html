2006-06-05 12:42:16 Matt Rudge CSSCGC Crap Games Contest 2006
Well, it's only a month since the CSSCGC 2006 website went live, but
we're already in double figures (well... 10, anyway) for submitted
games.

Just a reminder to send games to: …@… in an
emulator-friendly format.

As a slight correction to a previous post, not quite *all* Sinclair
machines are included.  Just the ZX80, ZX81, Spectrum, QL, (Z88 if you
want) and Zike :)

With regards to the judging, the plan is to open it to the public vote
(in the spirit of reality TV), with a guest judge as an adjudicator and
someone to have the final say in the event of a tie.

Think that about covers it.  I'll get back to work now!

-- 
Matt
