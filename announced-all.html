About this list

comp.sys.sinclair release announcements (1994–2014) about software or hardware for Sinclair machines that the index could not match to a ZXDB entry by title. Each one was checked by hand against ZXDB on 25 Sep 2026 (by Claude, reading the post and searching ZXDB by title, alternative titles, author and publisher). ZXDB copy: ~/projects/zx-spectrum/zxdb/zxdb.sqlite of 18 Sep 2026. The same verdicts as data: announcements-checked.tsv.

The other 47 of the 150 posts are left out of this list (25 Sep 2026): 41 turned out not to be Sinclair releases (PC software, news, jokes), and 6 could not be settled. They are in announcements-checked.tsv.

Several posts can be about the same product (e.g. the +3e ROMs from v0.87 to v1.30). The notes say what was searched; "no entry" means no title, alternative title, author or publisher match was found.
