2003-01-04 16:57:49 Andrew Broad ZXDB: Ma jolie
I shall say this only once. I have decided to release my new Manic Miner game 
"Ma jolie" in twenty weekly instalments, each containing one more room than the 
last. Room 0 ("Kari Weeds Off The Stragglers") was released on 1st January, Room 
1 will be released on 8th January, &c.

You will find each successive version of Ma jolie in the Files section of the 
Manic Miner and Jet Set Willy Yahoo! Group (you have to join the Group first, 
which prerequires signing up for a Yahoo! account and is free of charge).
